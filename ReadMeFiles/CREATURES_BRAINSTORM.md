# Creatures — Brainstorm (cover / index)

**Status: DRAFT / BRAINSTORM** (design chat, 2026-10-15).
This is the cover of the creature design document for
BioForge — species, behavior, temperament, taming, drops,
and biome homes. DESIGN-ONLY: it lives here, in the
ReadMeFiles, and becomes code in a later build phase, when
each entry converts to one row of a species data table.

**Why this file is short — the parts.** The single design
document outgrew the per-push size cap (~141K against
~131K), so by Adam's call (2026-10-15) it is kept in
**numbered parts** — one document, several files:

- **CREATURES_BRAINSTORM_1.md** — the framework: the
  creature matrix (Life x Locomotion x Shape), the three
  axes built out, the ten-field spectra template, the
  species-as-data build note — plus **roster passes 1-3
  (species #1-26)**: the ubiquitous layer, all family
  emblems, the first one-offs and hybrids.
- **CREATURES_BRAINSTORM_2.md** — **roster passes 4-9**
  (species #27-98): the new families' seats, hybrid
  waves, and the background casts.
- **CREATURES_BRAINSTORM_3.md** — **roster passes 10-16**
  (species #99-182): opened with the hundredth species
  (the Hearth Dog); holds the semi-ubiquitous
  foundation, the danger bench, the thin corners, the
  hybrid waves, and the water column.
- **CREATURES_BRAINSTORM_4.md** — **roster passes 17
  onward** (species #183-...): opened with the epic
  blanks and the epic distribution ruling.

New batches (of about twelve, per Adam's standing order)
append to the highest-numbered part; when it nears the
cap, the next numbered part opens and this index is
updated. Nothing is final until Adam's end review — names,
percentages, and placements are all revisable then.

**Not this document:** CREATURE_BEHAVIORS_BRAINSTORM.md —
the biome program's shipped-behavior stubs and flora
notes (this design inherits from it; it is a different
file, kept by the biome chat).

**Roster count:** 288 species designed (through the
twenty-second pass), plus the shipped cast already
in the game. The hybrid graph stands complete (55
of 55 pairings), the epic set is seated, the water
column and the alien rivers are populated; the
flora floods (48) and Fungus Flood One (24) are in,
and Fungus Flood Two follows in the standing
workstream. Layers complete so far: family emblems 11 of 11;
a one-off for every built biome variant; hybrid
creatures for 33 of 55 pairings; background casts under
way in every family; and the wide-range (semi-
ubiquitous) layer founded at 12 species across six
sense-drawn ranges — Adam's layer, so every biome
holds more than its emblem and its unique. Caves come
after the overworld, by Adam's sequencing.
