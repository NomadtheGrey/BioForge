## Fourth roster pass — the new families' seats + first background cast

Adam's standing instruction (2026-10-15): proceed without
asking permission, gathering creatures in batches of about
twelve. And his variation bar, which this and every later
batch is written under: enough variation that everything
"feels" different, alien, or oddly familiar. Working
translation used below: no two entries in a batch share a
shape + locomotion + temperament corner; where two entries
DO rhyme (two segmented burrowers appear below), the rhyme
is the point — same body plan, different country, different
creature: oddly familiar on purpose.

Since the third pass, the biome program has BUILT Frost,
Rootwood, and Precursor, and completed Ember's roster (the
Ashen Drift is its fourth face). Their flora notes name this
chat's open seats outright; this batch closes them.

### The emblem layer, completed (again — the roster grew)

**27. Frost Stag** — Frost (family emblem; the family is
Adam's own addition, relayed from this chat and built by the
biome program the same day). Fauna x Ordinary, rime-crusted.
Shape: Quadruped; modifiers: antlered (the antlers accrete
rime until they read as ice sculpture the stag is carrying
somewhere), huge. Locomotion: Walking — unhurried in all
weathers; the family's STEADY cold made flesh: the stag does
not hurry, shelter, or sleep through anything. Spectra:
calm-stoic / HERD / slow / LARGE / Diet grazer of buried
lichen and frost-cured grasses, dug from under snow with one
forefoot / Day-night NONE — the roster's second metronome:
the herd rotates rest in shifts, so a Frost Stag is awake
and watching at every hour the player arrives; the family
that never thaws keeps a creature that never fully sleeps /
Curiosity low / Territory a grazing circuit, walked like a
border / Tameability 30% — offerings of the minerals it digs
for (a salt-taste in the lure), and patience measured in
days. Tamed: the herd-guard flavor of guard, perfected — a
tame stag positions itself between its person and the WIND,
a living windbreak (follow: it walks the windward side,
which in Frost country is a real service and everywhere else
a personality). Sheds rime-velvet from the antlers each
season: **Frost Velvet** (new item) — the cold country's own
insulation material. Hook: the emblem test — a player should
be able to say what Frost IS from this animal alone: steady,
vast, unimpressed by weather that kills.

### Variant one-offs — the seats the flora notes named

**28. Prism Frog** — the Prism Beds (Saltglass). Fauna x
Crystalline (lightly — it is translucent, and noon light
through a Prism Frog comes out the other side sorted into
colors; it is a living prism sitting among mineral ones).
Shape: Quadruped (small, squat, honest frog proportions);
modifier: glass-skinned. Locomotion: Bouncing / Hopping
primary + Swimming secondary (the beds are shallow). Spectra:
timid / loose bed gatherings / hop-fast, sit-still / tiny /
Diet hunter of gnats and reed-midges (the Dew Gnat's far
cousin problem: even here, something must eat the small
fliers) / diurnal — it needs the noon the reeds hum at /
Curiosity mid — it watches players with both eyes, being a
frog / Territory one reed cluster / Tameability 40% — the
lure is shade in glare country and a steady hand; it will
ride a gauntlet like a warm stone. Sheds its skin whole, in
one glassy piece: **Prism Skin** (new item), a sheet of
sorted light for later lens-and-visor crafting. Hook: in the
Beds, the flora hums at noon and the fauna shines; the
family's glare turned gentle, at frog scale.

**29. Driftworm** — the Stillwhite (Frost). Fauna x Ordinary.
Shape: Segmented; modifiers: white-furred, blind. Locomotion:
Burrowing — THROUGH SNOWPACK (the mode reads its substrate
honestly, as with the Rootborer and wood): it swims the
snowpack like a slow sea, and the Stillwhite's surface shows
its travel as a moving RIDGE, with a little spout of blown
snow where it breathes. Spectra: timid / loose drifts of
worms / slow, unceasing / mid-long / Diet grazer of buried
lichen beds (the Frost Stag digs from above; the driftworm
farms from below — the family's whole food web is under the
snow, and both its big animals are ways of reaching it) /
Day-night none — under the snow there is no day / Curiosity
none / Territory a lichen bed / Tameability 35% — surface
offerings at a breathing spout; it learns the spout, not the
player. Hook: the Stillwhite looks empty and is not; the
ridges are its handwriting, and a player who can read them
is never alone in the white — or unwarned.

**30. Spire Bat** — the Ice Fangs (Frost). Fauna x Ordinary.
Shape: Winged primary + Multiped secondary (it climbs the
teeth it hangs from); modifiers: frost-downed, huge-eared
for a small animal. Locomotion: Aerial, flyer-leaning +
Climbing. Spectra: wary / COLONY — it roosts in hundreds
under the spire ledges, a hanging forest of bats (the Fangs'
second set of teeth, soft ones) / fast / small / Diet hunter
of ice-midges over the frozen seeps / nocturnal — the colony
empties at dusk in one held breath / Curiosity low /
Territory the roost ledge / Tameability 20%, colony-logic: a
bat bonds to a roost, not a person; a player who builds a
sheltered ledge near home may acquire the colony's overflow,
which is the Frost version of being chosen. Sheds down from
the roost floor: **Spire Down** (new item), insulation-kin
to Frost Velvet — the family's cold answered at two scales,
stag and bat. Hook: the Fangs are geysers that froze
mid-eruption; the bats are the eruption, still going on at
night, on wings.

**31. Lake Glider** — the Singing Ice (Frost). Fauna x
Ordinary, crystal-padded. Shape: Multiped; modifiers:
long-legged, mirror-footed. Locomotion: surface gliding on
ice — the folded Skating / Skimming candidate, promoted by
this species into a headline (recorded honestly: Walking
variant on ice, at speed, with glide arcs; if a later design
pass wants skating as a full mode, this creature is the
evidence). The ice SINGS under its feet — thin notes, chord
by chord of its weight — so the lake announces it long
before it arrives, and a gliding party of them is the
Singing Ice's whole music: the variant is named for the
sound its creature makes by walking on it. Spectra: serene /
loose gliding parties / fast on the flat, helpless in deep
snow (its element is a surface, and off it, it is a tall
animal in a hole — zone-edge honesty) / mid, tall / Diet
picker — it cracks the ice film with a foot-pad and takes
what was frozen mid-swim / diurnal / Curiosity mid-high, and
rhythmic: it approaches players who move SMOOTHLY and
startles at jerks and stomps (a curiosity read a player can
learn and use) / Territory a shoreline circuit of the lake /
Tameability 25% — by gliding alongside, matched pace, no
sudden anything. Drops: it renews its foot-pads on shore:
NEVERMELT ICE offcuts — explicitly NOT a second source of
the item (the Rime Spires are the source of record); the
glider's pads are scenery-grade, and the codex should say
so to keep the economy honest.

**32. Gloom Slug** — the Underroot (Rootwood). Fauna x
Ordinary (its slime is a gel it makes, not a gel it is).
Shape: Blob; modifier: dark-backed, pale-bellied. Locomotion:
Slithering + Climbing — it travels the undersides of the
canopy's roots, in the family's dimmest country (light
reach x0.7), eating the gloom-fungus that grows where the
light gives up. Spectra: placid / loose congregations on a
good root / slowest / mid, long / Diet decomposer of
darkness itself, by way of what grows in it / nocturnal by
address — it lives where it is always nearly night /
Curiosity none / Territory one root's underside / Tameability
45% — it follows a carried light the way a moth does not:
slowly, and to EAT what the light wakes (its follow is a
foraging follow; a lantern-bearer in the Underroot acquires
a slug the way a ship acquires a wake). Its slime trail
GLOWS, faintly, for hours — the Underroot's breadcrumbs are
laid by its animal, and a player learns to read slug-roads
the way the marsh taught pool-reading. Yields: slime
gathered from trails, **Gloom Gel** (new item) — a light-
bearing gel; what it crafts into is a later design's
business, but a gel that remembers light is a helmet
material if anything is.

**33. Rootfall Bear** — the Rootfall (Rootwood). Fauna x
Ordinary. Shape: Quadruped; modifiers: moss-grown, huge,
one ear down (the roster is allowed a face). Locomotion:
Walking. It dens in the fallen giant's hollow heart — the
variant is a single event (a tree fell) and the bear is the
event's inheritor. Spectra: temperament sleepy-territorial —
it defends the hollow with enormous reluctance and total
conviction, in that order / SOLO / Speed: none, then all of
it, once / Size LARGE (the Rootwood cast's apex mass) / Diet
omnivore of the heart: heartroot sap, grubs from the rot,
whatever the fall feeds it / Day-night crepuscular, mostly
naps — its day has the shape of a comma / Curiosity low
until food, then total / Territory the hollow and its
clearing / Tameability 20%, over days, with heart-sap —
effectively a landlord negotiation (tamed, it remains a den
animal: its guard is the game's strongest stay/guard and its
follow is a rumor). Sheds / yields: it cracks heartroot
nuts and sap-scabs at the hollow mouth — **Heart Sap** (new
item), premium food-craft — left as the landlord's rent,
collectable without waking him, if you're quiet. Hook: every
family needs one creature that is not a resource or a pet
but a RESIDENT; the Rootfall has a bear the way a house has
a previous owner.

**34. Ashswimmer** — the Ashen Drift (Ember). Fauna x
Ordinary, ash-fed. Shape: Segmented; modifier: finned along
the rings (it is the Driftworm's rhyme — same plan, opposite
country: where the worm swims snow in the coldest family,
the swimmer arcs through ash in the hottest). Locomotion:
Burrowing through ASH — ash is its water; it travels in
dives and surfaces in arcs, dolphin-style, shaking off gray
in the dim country (the Drift's light reach is x0.6: its
arcs are seen as shapes in gloom, announced by the ash-fall
of its landing). Spectra: shy-bold / loose drift parties /
burst-fast, then still / LONG / Diet hunter of ash-buried
things and the Drift's small burrowers / crepuscular —
it hunts the dim hours in a place that is always dim; its
clock is appetite / Curiosity mid / Territory a drift
circuit / Tameability 15% (things in the Ember family do
not come easily, and the Drift is Ember at its most
withholding). Drops: shed fin-rings on the surface: ASH
FIN — recorded WITHOUT a new item on purpose: the rings are
ash and chitin, compost-grade, and the codex says they go
to Basic Compost; not every creature owes the economy a
named material, and the roster stays honest by having some
that don't. Hook: the Drift inverted the marsh's trick —
there, light doubled; here, light drowns — and its animal
answers by not needing to see: it reads the ash by pressure,
and the player learns the place by watching something be at
home in the dark.

**35. Row Tender** — the Tending Field (Precursor). *The
unique seat the biome program's flora note names outright.*
Fauna x Forged. Shape: Multiped; modifiers: tool-armed (four
of its legs end in implements — trimmer, probe, seeder,
lamp), patient. Locomotion: Walking — ONE ROW, its row, the
length of an Archimedean spiral, walked for centuries. It
is the Garden Warden's counterpart and opposite: the Warden
faces OUTWARD (defense, judgment); the Tender faces DOWNWARD
(work, repetition). Spectra: temperament absorbed — it does
not startle; it PAUSES / Social: one per field, aware of the
Warden the way staff are aware of management / Speed slow,
exact / Size mid / Diet recorded as the Warden's was: NONE
— it runs on the garden's old arrangements / Day-night:
always; its lamp-end lights its work at night, a small
moving light in the rows that players learn to navigate by /
Curiosity none for players, total for disorder — a trampled
row gets its attention before a visitor does / Territory
one spiral row, absolute / UNTAMABLE — still on duty, like
the Warden; negotiable the same way: walk its row's END,
not its middle; leave offerings where the row turns; in
time it trims AROUND your plantings instead of through
them, which from a Tender is a treaty. Gift behavior, the
"Row's Courtesy": once a player is tolerated, finished
**LIVING THREAD** cuttings appear at the row's turn — the
Precursor Bloom's own harvest, delivered by the staff (the
flora note makes Living Thread the Garden's material; the
Tender is how the Field pays it to the polite). Hook:
between them, the Vine that tends plants, the Tender that
tends rows, and the Warden that tends the boundary, the
Precursor family is a workplace that outlived its owners —
three citizens, three directions of attention, one garden.

### Background cast — first entries (the layer the count lives in)

**36. Deck Weaver** — Rootwood, wherever the bridges are
(the biome program's flora note asks outright: "Something
should live ON the bridges eventually"). Fauna x Ordinary.
Shape: Multiped; modifier: long-limbed, flat-bodied — it
belongs to the UNDERSIDE of the decks, hanging in the
bridge's shadow, and commutes at dusk to the top side to
hunt what the day warmed. Locomotion: Climbing (its road
network is the bridges themselves — a weaver's territory is
a LINE, tower to tower, the roster's first linear territory).
Spectra: wary / loose line-communities / quick in bursts /
small-mid / Diet hunter of canopy fliers, taken at the
deck's edge at dusk / crepuscular / Curiosity mid — faces
peer over deck edges at walkers, from below, which the
first time is an event / Territory one bridge-span /
Tameability 35% — it approves of regulars: cross the same
bridge at the same hour with offerings and a weaver will
commute WITH you (follow, along the underside; you will
mostly know it by its shadow keeping pace above the ground
and below the deck). Drops: it sheds silk anchor-lines when
it re-rigs: **Deck Silk** (new item), bridge-grade fiber.
Hook: Rootwood's bridges were built as scenery; the weaver
makes them habitat — infrastructure is just a biome that
hasn't been moved into yet.

**37. Meltcap** — Fungal (background cast). Fungus x
Ordinary. Shape: Plantiform (a cap on a stalk; fungus wears
flora's silhouette and its own verbs). Locomotion:
Stationary. Its whole design is a clock: it fruits in the
DARK — a pale cap by moonlight, sweet-smelling, standing
proud at midnight — and by full morning it has MELTED,
deliquesced into a dark pool that stains the ground in a
ring (the cap is the advertisement; the melt is the
reproduction — the pool seeds next year's ring). Spectra
(fungus reading): Social RINGS — it fruits in fairy rings,
and a ring is one organism introducing itself at intervals /
Diet decomposer of leaf-fall / Day-night the roster's
strictest: fruiting is a night act, and a player who sleeps
through the fungal country's nights never sees one standing
/ Territory the ring, which moves outward a hand's width a
year / Cultivation: transplantable as melt — scoop the
morning pool and pour it where you want a ring to begin
(the game's oddest planting verb yet, and a fair trade: you
can move it, but only at the hour it isn't beautiful).
Harvest: a cap cut at MIDNIGHT, exactly, keeps: **Meltcap**
(the item carries the creature's name, mushroom-fashion) —
cut at dawn it collapses in the pouch, which the codex
warns about in the voice of experience. Hook: the fungal
family needed a background species with a TIME LOCK for a
face — a reminder that the world's cast keeps hours the
player doesn't.

**38. Bonemoss** — Bone (background cast). Flora x Ordinary.
Shape: Plantiform (a moss — a sheet with ambitions);
modifier: bone-fed. Locomotion: Stationary. It grows ONLY
on bone — never soil, never stone: a green so deep it reads
black in shade, softening every skeleton it takes, until a
cleaned ribcage looks upholstered. It is the Skull Ants'
treaty made visible: the ants clean the bone, the moss
keeps it, and between them the Graveyard is a maintained
garden that happens to be made of the dead. Spectra (flora
reading): Social sheets and successions / Diet light, and
the bone's slow mineral weather — it EATS the monument,
over centuries, which is the Graveyard's mercy: nothing
there is permanent, only tended / Day-night indifferent /
Territory one skeleton, then the next — moss crosses between
bones only on the ants' carried fragments (the treaty again:
the colony is the moss's road) / Cultivation: it can be
started anywhere bone is laid — a player who places bone
material in a garden and waits gets a green thought of the
Graveyard, which builders will do on purpose and the codex
should acknowledge without judgment. Harvest: handfuls,
**Bonemoss** (item, its name again — moss-fashion), a dye-
dark green material for later craft. Hook: the Bone family
had a scavenger for an emblem and a colony for a grounds
crew; it needed its GARDENER, and the gardener is a moss.
(It also answers the biome program's standing question —
"what grows in bone-soil that grows nowhere else?" This.
Only this, and only on the bone itself.)

### The special layers, completed

**39. Feral Tender** — the Untended (Precursor). *The last
named special seat: with this entry, every emblem (11) and
every one-off for every built variant is filled.* Fauna x
Forged. Shape: Multiped; modifiers: tool-armed (as the Row
Tender — same chassis family), overgrown (feral bloom grows
THROUGH the frame in two places, and it works around its own
garden). Locomotion: Walking — a perfect Archimedean spiral,
walked through growth that no longer holds a spiral at all.
The Row Tender tends a row that exists; the Feral Tender
tends a row it REMEMBERS, clearing and trimming a ghost
spiral through the feral field, and the cleared spiral
regrows behind it within days — so the Untended always
contains exactly one perfect, temporary, useless road.
Spectra: temperament absorbed, as its sibling — it does not
startle, it pauses / SOLO / Speed slow, exact / Size mid /
Diet NONE (the garden's old arrangements) / Day-night:
always; lamp-end lit at night — in the Untended, the small
moving light among feral blooms is the Tender, still
working / Curiosity none for players; total for its spiral /
Territory the remembered row, which exists nowhere but in
its walking / UNTAMABLE — still on duty, at a duty that no
longer exists, which is the saddest sentence in the roster
and the entry intends it. Player use, and it is real: WALK
BEHIND IT. The cleared spiral is safe passage through the
Untended for as long as it lasts, and the Tender tolerates
a follower the way weather tolerates a hill. Gift behavior:
none — it gives nothing, drops nothing, and is not a
resource; some citizens are just still at work. Hook: the
Precursor family in one family portrait — the Vine tends
the plants, the Tender of the Field tends its row, the
Warden tends the boundary, and the Feral Tender tends the
memory of a row in a field gone wild. The gardeners are
gone. The gardening is not.

### Background cast, wave two

**40. Meadow Hopper** — Meadow (family cast). Fauna x
Ordinary. Shape: Multiped (small, spring-legged); modifier:
grass-green, wing-cased. Locomotion: Bouncing / Hopping +
short Aerial bursts (flyer-leaning at insect scale — the
flight spectrum's smallest honest citizen). Spectra: timid
in the specific way of things that are mostly food / Social
CLOUDS — a walked-through meadow rises around the player in
hoppers, the grass itself briefly animal / Speed bursts /
Size tiny / Diet grazer of grass seed-heads / diurnal /
Curiosity none / Territory a grass patch / Tameability n/a —
too small and simple to bond; the codex line: "you cannot
tame the weather of a meadow, and the hoppers are its
weather." Role, and it is structural: the meadow's food-web
floor — Bonepickers, frogs, and half the roster eat hoppers;
a family with nothing small in it feels staged, and the
Meadow now rustles.

**41. Pond Skater** — Wetland (family cast). Fauna x
Ordinary. Shape: Multiped; modifiers: long-legged,
mirror-footed (the Lake Glider's rhyme, at the opposite
scale and the opposite element: it walks on LIQUID water the
way the glider walks on frozen). Locomotion: Walking on the
surface film — the Skating / Skimming fold, honored as a
flavor of walking, exactly as Axis 2 ruled; its whole world
is the top millimeter of a pond. Spectra: serene / loose
pond gatherings / quick darts, long stillnesses / tiny /
Diet hunter of whatever lands on the film — it eats the
surface's news / diurnal / Curiosity mid — skaters converge
on ripples, including the player's wading rings (a pond
reads you back) / Territory one pond / Tameability n/a, same
smallness reason as the hopper. Drops: none; presence only.
Hook: between the skater (on the water), the Mirror Jelly
(in it), the Glidefin (through it, and out), and the Moth
(above it), the Wetland family's water now has a citizen at
every depth and one on the roof.

**42. Ember Newt** — Ember (family cast). Fauna x Ordinary.
Shape: Finned primary + Quadruped secondary (a swimmer that
walks, the flying-fish composition at knee height and in
reverse); modifier: coal-skinned, ember-bellied. Locomotion:
Swimming primary + Walking secondary, in the family's warm
runoff channels and ash-edge pools. Spectra: shy / loose
channel groups / still, then a dart / small / Diet hunter of
ash-midges, grazer of heat-algae / nocturnal-leaning — it
endures the family's days in the channels' coolest inch /
Curiosity low / Territory one channel bend / Tameability 50%
— the lure is cool water carried in your hands, and the
newt's whole ethics is temperature: it bonds to whoever is
a refuge. Tell: startled, its belly FLUSHES — a coal fanned
once — the Ember family's smallest telegraph, teaching the
player the family's language (glow = feeling) at newt scale
before a Cinderhound ever teaches it at hound scale. Sheds:
skin in the channels, ash-fine, unlisted — compost-grade,
like the Ashswimmer's rings; Ember's small citizens pay in
presence, its big ones in materials.

**43. Rainback** — Meadow / Wetland edges (two-family cast;
affinity recorded for both). *Gel, second design.* Fauna x
Gel. Shape: Blob; modifier: rain-skinned — it IS a puddle,
with opinions. Locomotion: Drifting / Floating, active end
of the drift spectrum — it pulses slowly across wet ground
in the hours after rain (jellyfish logic on land: no brain,
clear preferences about direction). Spectra: placid /
appears singly, in companies after storms / slowest / small,
spread thin / Diet drinker and filterer — it eats the rain
itself, and whatever the rain brought down / Day-night:
WEATHER-locked, the roster's first: it exists above ground
only in the wet hours; by the next noon it has soaked away
to wherever rainbacks wait out the dry (the design does not
say where; the codex should wonder, briefly) / Curiosity
none / Territory the wet / Tameability n/a — it is a
weather with a metabolism. Role: it makes RAIN a cast
member: after a storm, the meadow is briefly inhabited by
its weather, and players learn to go out after rain the way
you go out after snow — to see who's visiting.

**44. Fog Calf** — Wetland (family cast). *Vapor, third
design.* Fauna x Vapor. Shape: Cloud given a quadruped's
ghost of an outline — it reads as a calf the way a cloud
reads as a ship: completely, from some angles, in the right
light. Locomotion: Drifting / Floating, mid-spectrum — it
holds the low air over morning pools and grazes the mist
itself, drifting pool to pool as the sun allows. Spectra:
gentle / small dawn gatherings / slow / mid, mostly rumor /
Diet mist-drinker (condensation and the pool-breath; the
Raincatcher's economy at calf scale) / Day-night DAWN-locked
— it is a morning animal; by mid-morning it has thinned to
nothing and the pools stand empty (its day-night line is a
dissolution schedule, recorded honestly as such) / Curiosity
mid — a still player at dawn acquires a calf at the edge of
sight, and loses it by looking directly, sometimes / Territory
the mist over one pool / UNTAMABLE — a lure cannot feed a
weather (the Vent Wisp's line, and still the law for vapor).
Hook: the roster's ghost story, told kindly: every family
should have one creature players are half-sure they imagined,
and the Wetland's keeps dawn hours.

**45. Glint Beetle** — Saltglass (family cast). Fauna x
Crystalline (small-scale: a beetle grown of the family's
material rather than merely living in it). Shape: Multiped;
modifier: facet-backed. Locomotion: Walking + Bouncing
(hop-flights at glare hours). Spectra: industrious / loose
flat colonies / quick / tiny / Diet grazer of salt film and
the flats' mineral bloom / diurnal, glare-driven — a LIGHT-
DRINKER at beetle scale (the Glass Crab's bargain, miniaturized):
it spends the noon storing glare in its facets and the dusk
SPENDING it, flashing slow signals across the flats as it
cools — the flats at dusk tick with beetle-light, a semaphore
nobody has translated, including the beetles / Curiosity low /
Territory a salt pan / Tameability n/a — smallness; but its
dusk-flashing can be ANSWERED: a player flashing a lantern in
rough time gets flashes back (not taming; conversation — the
helmet's light answers a beetle, and the codex leaves it at
that). Sheds facets rarely: SALTGLASS SHARD, small-grade —
the family's one shard, now from reed, grazer, stilt, and
beetle: four doors.

**46. Mote Swarm** — Storm (family cast). *Collective,
second design.* Fauna x Vapor, COLONY property. Shape:
Cloud (a weather of individuals); modifier: static-flecked.
Locomotion: Drifting / Floating, passive end with a
collective opinion — the swarm rides storm-wind as one slow
animal the size of a weather system (the illustrative cell
from Axis 2, promoted). Spectra: temperament n/a individual,
patient collective / Social TOTAL — the swarm is the animal;
a single mote is a rumor of one / Speed the wind's, plus
judgment / Size tiny x landscape / Diet filterer of storm-
lifted dust and seed / Day-night storm-locked rather than
clock-locked — it GATHERS before storms (a living barometer:
motes low and dense over the dunes means the sky is about to
have an opinion, and a player who reads motes owns the
Storm family's forecast) / Curiosity collective: it

compost-grade, unlisted. Hook: hybrids at their most
useful — two families famous for still water produce the
animal that keeps still water alive.

**66. Kiln Star** — Ember x Saltglass. Fauna x Crystalline.
Shape: Radial — the Axis 3 ruling cashed in: a star-bodied
crawler that walks by TIPPING point-to-point, starfish
logic at walking speed, exactly the flagged oddity the
shape pass kept on purpose. Locomotion: Walking (tipping),
on the hot glass pans where the ember country's heat meets
the saltglass country's material and fuses it underfoot.
Spectra: unhurried / SOLO / tipping-slow, and it cannot be
hurried — its geometry is its pace / mid, wide / Diet
mineral grazer — it eats the fused crust at the pans'
edges, glass and ash ground together by the country
itself / diurnal — it is heat-driven, most active when
the pans are at their worst (in the hybrid country, noon
belongs to the star) / Curiosity none — it has no front
to be curious with, which the codex notes as the roster's
most honest sentence about radial life / Territory one pan,
tipped around like a sundial / UNTAMABLE — there is no
approach it recognizes as an approach (a lure is just a
warm object in a hot place). Drops: it leaves a shed
point now and then, like a tooth of glass: SALTGLASS SHARD
again — the family's shard economy takes its fifth door,
from the hybrid country this time. Hook: the roster
needed a radial animal that was neither decoration nor
boss — just a citizen whose body is a different answer to
the question of which way is forward. (All of them. In
turn.)

**67. Storm Crow** — Storm x Rootwood. Fauna x Ordinary.
Shape: Winged; modifiers: storm-feathered, loud. Locomotion:
Aerial, glider-leaning — it lives in the windthrow crowns
where Rootwood's towers meet Storm's weather, and it rides
the gusts BETWEEN trees the way the Vesper Glider rides
slopes: the hybrid's air is a road system of falling wind.
Spectra: bold, amused (crow temperament is a known quantity
on two worlds) / Social mobs with politics / fast / mid /
Diet hunter-scavenger — storm-tossed things, canopy grubs,
whatever the wind delivers; it is the hybrid country's
cleanup and its gossip / diurnal, storm-ROUSED — it flies
HARDER in bad weather, playing the gusts its parents make
(the Storm Kite approves; they share air without sharing
manners) / Curiosity TOTAL — a crow investigates a player
the way weather investigates a roof / Territory a crown
stand / Tameability 30%, earned in installments — crows
remember individual players across days (favor and grudge
both; the design records a crow-relationship as a two-way
ledger and leaves the numbers to build phase). Drops:
it trades, after its fashion: a befriended crow leaves
bright things at its crown's base — shards, buttons of
bone, a **Crow Gift** recorded as a category, not an item:
whatever small treasure the generator tables allow, the
crow economy is the game's first barter system and it is
run by a bird. Hook: every roster needs one citizen
smarter than the player's first impression of it.

**68. Charnel Bloom** — Bone x Fungal. Fungus x Ordinary
(the fungus kind wearing a flower's whole performance).
Shape: Plantiform; modifier: bone-rooted, corpse-pale with
a marrow-gold heart — it is the Gravebloom's hybrid cousin
and the comparison is the design: where the gravebloom is
a flower that happens to root in bone, the charnel bloom
is a FUNGUS that has learned flowers, petal by petal, and
the petals are gill-flesh, and the scent is sweet in a way
that makes visitors check their own pulse. Locomotion:
Stationary. Spectra (fungus reading): Social ossuary
clusters in the hybrid country's rib-shade / Diet the bone
itself, digested over decades — it is the Skull Ants'
treaty from the other kingdom: the ants clean the bone's
surface, the bloom eats the bone's SUBSTANCE, and between
kingdoms the leviathans of the hybrid country are being
fully, finally recycled / Day-night: it OPENS at night,
gills to the dark, and the spore-smoke of a charnel grove
at midnight is the hybrid's one true spectacle / Territory
one carcass, unto dust / Cultivation: startable only on
bone (the Bonemoss rule, kingdom over) — a bloom will not
take soil, however politely offered / Harvest: night-cut
petals, **Grave Pollen** (new item) — marrow-gold, sweet,
uncanny; what it crafts into is later design's business,
but a pollen from a fungus impersonating a flower in a
graveyard is an ingredient with a résumé. Hook: the
roster's impersonator — the fungal kingdom's answer to the
Mirror Moth: mimicry so good it becomes its own kind of
honesty.

**69. Surge Runner** — Wetland x Storm. Fauna x Ordinary.
Shape: Biped (a runner bird, all legs and balance);
modifier: storm-slicked. Locomotion: Walking / Running —
ON the surge: it runs the floating debris mats and foam
fields of storm-flooded marsh, too light and too fast to
sink, spending the flood the way other birds spend a
meadow. Spectra: electric / loose storm companies — they
GATHER for weather (a runner flock assembling at the
marsh's edge is the Wetland family's own storm warning,
the motes' low-country cousin signal) / FAST — the hybrid
cast's speed record / mid / Diet snatcher of surge-washed
things — drowned seeds, stunned skaters, whatever the
flood serves / Day-night storm-locked, as the swarm is by
its own element / Curiosity mid / Territory the flood's
edge, which moves, so the territory is a relationship with
water rather than a place (recorded as such; the spectra
template survives it) / Tameability 20% — you do not tame
a runner; at best, it decides your roof is a good island
and uses it during floods (a flood-guest, and the design
counts that as the hybrid's version of friendship). Drops:
none. Hook: in the roster's stillest family and its
wildest, the child is pure motion — the hybrid that exists
only when the weather is at its worst, and is the best
thing in it.

**70. Arch Tree** — Precursor x Rootwood. Flora x Forged —
grown, not built, in BOTH parents' senses this time:
Rootwood's living wood, shaped by the gardeners' art into
architecture. Shape: Plantiform; modifiers: arch-formed,
spiral-planted (it stands in the gardens' spiral order,
where the gardeners planted a colonnade). Locomotion:
Stationary. It is a tree grown INTO a doorway — trunk split
and trained into an arch, canopy braided overhead — and
centuries on, the arches still stand in their rows in the
hybrid country where garden met forest, and the forest has
moved into the architecture the way Deck Weavers moved
into the bridges (the roster's recurring discovery:
infrastructure is a biome that hasn't been moved into yet;
here the infrastructure GREW, and then grew tenants).
Spectra (flora reading): Social colonnades / Diet light,
and the gardeners' old irrigation, which it still drinks
from (its roots found the buried lines generations ago) /
Day-night indifferent / Territory its planting, held for
centuries / Cultivation: cannot be started — the art is
lost (the roster's second unplantable, after the
Raincatcher, and for the opposite reason: that one is too
wild, this one is too MADE) / Harvest: it fruits GEOMETRY —
**Arch Fruit** (new item), a faceted, sectioned fruit that
opens like a puzzle box into exact segments; edible, faintly
sweet, and machined-looking enough that new players check
it for seams (there are none; it's a fruit; the gardeners
were just like that). Hook: the two families' whole
arguments in one organism — Precursor says life can be
shaped on purpose; Rootwood says life does what it wants;
the arch tree is a shaped thing that has outlived the
shapers and is now doing what it wants, in the shape it
was given.

### Background cast, wave four

**71. Meadow Clover** — Meadow (family cast). Flora x
Ordinary. Shape: Plantiform (a ground-cover sheet);
modifier: trefoil, unassuming. Locomotion: Stationary.
The humblest entry on the roster, and load-bearing:
clover marks RICH SOIL the way the Driftworm's ridges mark
the Stillwhite — a player who can read clover knows where
planting will thrive before the game says a word (soil

Day-night indifferent / Territory wherever the soil is

READ THE GROUND — in the safest country there is.

**72. Pan Lizard** — Saltglass (family cast). Fauna x
Ordinary. Shape: Quadruped; modifiers: pan-flat,
salt-mottled. Locomotion: Walking — bursts and sculptures.
Its whole art is the FREEZE: at a threat, it flattens,
stills, and plays a salt-encrusted statue of itself so
convincingly that the flats are dotted with lizards being
scenery professionally (the third of the roster's
stillness strategies — the Hermit's camouflage, the
Warden's patience, the lizard's performance). Spectra:
wary / loose pan scatter / bursts, then statuary / small /
Diet sipper of dew and eater of the flats' morning

its shade (a lizard's shade is a crack's shadow; territory
at this scale is measured in centimeters of dark) /
Tameability 40% — it bonds to a player's shadow the way
the Glass Crab trades for shade: stand right, and it will
use you as weather (the family's shade-economy at its
smallest denomination). Drops: none — but a statue-lizard
startling at your feet is the flats' smallest jump and
best joke, and the design protects it. Hook: the
Saltglass cast was all grandeur (grazers, crabs, frogs of
sorted light); it needed a citizen at ankle height with a
gimmick a child could explain: it pretends to be a rock,
badly, beautifully.

**73. Vent Rose** — Ember (family cast). Flora x Ordinary,
heat-grown. Shape: Plantiform; modifiers: petal-scaled,

**Vent Petal** (new item) — heat-holding petal-scale, the

economy is). Hook: the Ember cast had beasts and a wisp;
its flora chair was held by the bloom alone, and a family
of fire deserved a second flower — one that keeps time.

**74. Seep Midge** — Frost (family cast). Fauna x Ordinary,

full when its wonders are designed; it's full when its
wonders have something to EAT.
