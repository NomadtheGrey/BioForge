## Tenth roster pass — the hundredth species

Part 3 opens where the count demanded: two entries from
the ends of the Size spectrum, saved to stand together —
the largest creature on the roster, and the one that was
always waiting at the player's own door.

**99. Grass Leviathan** — the far plains (Meadow x Storm
country and the deep Meadow beyond it; affinity recorded
for both). Fauna x Ordinary. Shape: Quadruped; modifiers:
hill-backed — its back grows MEADOW, a walking acre of
grass, clover, and whatever has rooted in it (Tumble Sages
anchor on it seasonally; meadow hoppers winter in its
grass; it is the roster's first WALKABLE creature, and the
design records that plainly: you can stand on it, and it
does not mind, and it does not notice). Locomotion: Walking
— its stride period is minutes; its migration is a season;
from the ground it reads as weather arriving (the grass
bends ahead of it for a mile, and herds travel in its wake
eating what its passage uncovered). Spectra: abyssally
calm / SOLO, with an entourage it never invited / Speed:
a horizon's / Size: the spectrum's top anchor — beyond
LARGE there is only this (the template records it as
LANDSCAPE, and the design accepts the new word once) /
Diet grazer of whole meadows, in passing / Day-night: it
sleeps standing, mid-stride, for days; the plains' caravans
time their camps to its naps / Curiosity none — at its
scale, a player is a rumor the grass tells / Territory a
migration, walked for centuries / UNTAMABLE — you cannot
tame geography; you can FOLLOW it, and generations of the
plains' creatures do (a leviathan-route is the far
country's oldest road: cleared, grazed, and renewed by
the walking itself). Drops: none taken; its shed winter
coat-slabs at the migration's end are meadow in brick
form, and the codex leaves them as scenery. Hook: every
roster of wonders needs one citizen that resets the
player's sense of size — the Bonefields keep a dead one
as a house; the plains keep a live one as a ROAD.

**100. Hearth Dog** — everywhere a player settles
(ubiquitous layer; the layer's fourth designed member).
Fauna x Ordinary. Shape: Quadruped (dog plan, mid-sized,
unremarkable in every proportion — deliberately: after
ninety-nine wonders, the hundredth is a DOG). Locomotion:
Walking. Its law, and the design states it as the species'
whole template entry in one breath: a hearth dog is not
found, caught, or tamed — it ARRIVES. When a base crosses
the line from camp to home (a hearth kept, a roof kept, a
garden kept — the game's own measures, numbers deferred),
a dog comes out of the country one morning and takes up
at the doorstep, the way the Hearth Wisp takes up at a
healthy fire: the wisp certifies the fire; the dog
certifies the HOME. Spectra: loyal, self-possessed /
Social: one per household, courteous to its kind / Speed:
a dog's / Size mid / Diet the household's leavings, by
right and by arrangement / Day-night: the household's /
Curiosity total, domestic / Territory the doorstep, the
yard, the walk to the fields — a perimeter it invents and
then keeps / Tameability: REVERSED — the template's one
inversion, recorded proudly: the bonding runs the other
way. Feed it three days, keep its hours, and it decides;
a decided dog is the roster's most complete pet (follow,
stay, and guard all at full fluency — it has been watching
how you live, and has views) — and a dog that has NOT
decided will leave one morning as it came, and the design
lets it, because a hundredth species should be allowed to
teach the roster's deepest rule by example: in BioForge,
even domestication is a relationship, not a mechanic.
Drops: none. It is not that kind of citizen. Hook: the
roster designed the world's edges first — its emblems,
its weathers, its dead gods' gardening staff — and saved
the doorstep for #100 on purpose. This is the animal
every player will know longest.

### Hybrid wave four

**101. Root Otter** — Wetland x Rootwood (place name
draft: the Drowned Roots). Fauna x Ordinary. Shape:
Quadruped; modifiers: sleek, long, unbothered by either
parent's element. Locomotion: Swimming primary + Climbing
secondary — it treats flooded root country as one
continuous medium (water, root, slide, water) and is the
hybrid's proof that the two families were always one
country waiting for its citizen. Spectra: playful,
professionally / small family rafts / quick / mid / Diet
hunter-picker of pool and root alike / diurnal / Curiosity
TOTAL — an otter investigates a player the way the Storm
Crow does, at closer range and with wetter hands: it
STEALS small bright things into root-caches (the crow's
barter economy has a wet branch; what the otter considers
payment is a logic of its own, and returned items appear
in caches like dividends) / Territory a root-and-pool
circuit / Tameability 40% — by playing along, over days.
Drops: its caches themselves — found caches hold **Otter
Treasure**, recorded as the Crow Gift's category-kin: a
category, not an item; the generator tables decide the
treasure, the otter decides the bank. Hook: the hybrid
casts needed a citizen whose whole body is an argument
that the border isn't real.

**102. Logback** — Fungal x Rootwood (place name draft:
the Rotwood). Fauna x Ordinary. Shape: Blob (slug plan,
grand scale); modifiers: bark-mimicking — its back is
indistinguishable from a rotting log until the log moves,
which it does, slowly, at night, leaving a fertilized
trail the forest uses by morning. Locomotion: Slithering
+ Burrowing secondary (through hollow logs — its road
network is the family's dead). Spectra: placid / loose
rot companies / slowest / LARGE for a slug, which the
template enjoys recording / Diet decomposer of heartwood
— it eats the Rootfall's slow future and the Underroot's
leavings / nocturnal / Curiosity none / Territory a
log-system / Tameability 30% — it approves of being rained
on together; a logback kept is a compost engine with a
commute (the Mulch Beetle's thesis, at slug scale and
with less paperwork). Drops: its trail, gathered, is
premium compost-grade — unlisted, per the house
discipline for honest small change. Hook: the Gloom Slug
eats darkness in the Underroot; the logback eats the
FOREST ITSELF in the Rotwood — the family's two slugs are
the two halves of its recycling, above the roots and
below them.

**103. Saltpicker** — Saltglass x Bone (place name draft:
the White Fields). Fauna x Ordinary. Shape: Winged
(bird plan, long-legged for a flier); modifiers:
white-mottled, patient. Locomotion: Aerial, flyer-leaning
+ Walking secondary — it commutes between the flats' glare
and the fields' fragments, and it BUILDS: a saltpicker's
nest is a small architecture of bone fragments and
saltglass, mortared with salt, unmistakable, and reused
for generations (the roster's second builder, after the
Bridges' weavers — nests as landmarks: a line of
saltpicker nests is the White Fields' only signage).
Spectra: methodical / mated pairs, colonial at the good
cliffs / mid / mid / Diet picker of marrow-film, salt
fleas, the flats' small dead / diurnal / Curiosity mid /
Territory a nest and its picking-ground / Tameability 25%
— they bond to a NEST-SITE, not a person (the Spire Bat's
colony rule at pair scale): keep a good cliff unmolested
near home and the pickers may annex it. Drops: failed
nests, salvaged, yield fragments unlisted; a lined nest's
fallen lining is **Picker Down** (new item) — white,
salt-stiff, the margin's blanket-stuff. Hook: two white
families meet and produce the roster's whitest citizen —
camouflage as a family resemblance.

**104. Stormcap** — Storm x Fungal (place name draft: the
Spore Storm country). *Fungus x Vapor — the travelling
kingdom, at full strangeness.* Fungus x Vapor. Shape:
Plantiform (a cap on a stalk, with the cap INFLATED);
modifier: gas-bellied. Locomotion: Stationary as a
mycelium — and Drifting / Floating as a FRUIT: at
maturity the cap detaches, inflates, and RIDES THE STORM
(the Poolcap's raft logic, airborne: the colony stays;
the fruit travels; countries are crossed at weather
speed, and a stormcap fall — hundreds of caps grounding
after one storm — is the hybrid country's sowing day,
marked by everything that eats them). Spectra (fungus
reading): Social storm-scatter / Diet decomposer of the
home country's fall, storm-fed / Day-night storm-locked —
fruiting follows the family's weather, not the clock /
Territory two territories, honestly recorded: the
mycelium's ground, and wherever the wind files the
caps / Cultivation: a grounded cap, kept, starts a
colony in a season (the roster's most lottery-like
planting: most caps feed the country; one in a hundred
is a forest) / Harvest: grounded caps before they root,
**Stormcap** (item, its name) — spiced, storm-cured,
the far country's provisions. Hook: the fungus kingdom
had clocks (Meltcap), sails (Poolcap), and impersonators
(Charnel Bloom); the stormcap is its EMIGRANT — the
kingdom's way of leaving home.

**105. Relay Wisp** — Precursor x Storm (the far margin).
Fauna x Forged — a precursor-made wisp, still running its
route. Shape: Cloud at lantern scale; modifier:
node-lit (it brightens at its route's turning points).
Locomotion: Drifting / Floating with PHASING-adjacent
habits — it travels a FIXED CIRCUIT between ruin sites
and stormcatcher stands, and at each node it is briefly,
entirely THERE, then a thread of light along the route,
then there again (recorded honestly: the circuit is
locomotion as SCHEDULE; if the Blink Hare is phasing by
nature, the wisp is phasing by TIMETABLE). Spectra:
punctual / SOLO per circuit, aware of other circuits the
way trains are / Speed the route's / small / Diet charge
and old instructions — it sips stormcatcher static and
garden current impartially / Day-night: always; it is
the far country's only reliable night-light, IF your hour
matches its round / Curiosity none — it has a route /
Territory the circuit, absolutely / UNTAMABLE — still on
duty, the Forged refrain; but a player who learns its
round owns a guide: follow the wisp's light and you walk
a precursor road older than the zones, node to node, in
country with no other roads. Drops: none. Hook: the
roster's lighthouse — a citizen whose whole use is that
it goes, reliably, where the player needs to go, and was
doing so before the player existed.

**106. Frost Newt** — Wetland x Frost (place name draft:
the Cold Marsh). Fauna x Ordinary. Shape: Finned primary
+ Quadruped secondary (the Ember Newt's composition, in
mirror). Locomotion: Swimming + Walking, in marsh water
that skins with ice by night. Spectra: shy / loose marsh
companies / still, then a dart (the newt sentence, and
the design lets it stand twice — the two newts are the
roster's controlled experiment) / small / Diet hunter of
the cold marsh's suspended life / crepuscular / Curiosity
low / Territory one marsh bend / Tameability 50% — the
lure is a hand's warmth held at the ice edge, the exact
inverse of its ember cousin's cool-water lure (the two
entries cross-reference deliberately: same animal, two
clash-margin countries, opposite currencies — temperature
is the newts' whole ethics, in both directions). Tell:
content, its throat PULSES pale blue — the cold family's
glow-answer to the ember belly-flush. Drops: shed skin,
unlisted. Hook: designed as a pair across two batches and
two families' argument — the roster's proof that a mirror
can be a citizen, not a copy.

**107. Pale Hare** — Meadow x Frost (place name draft:
the Pale Meadows). Fauna x Ordinary. Shape: Quadruped
(hare plan); modifiers: snow-white, long-eared (the Blink
Hare's ordinary cousin — no vapor, no ticks; the design
keeps the two hares in different weathers of the real).
Locomotion: Bouncing / Hopping + Walking. Spectra:
wary-gentle / loose meadow companies / burst-fast / small /
Diet grazer of frost-cured meadow grasses / crepuscular /
Curiosity mid / Territory a meadow circuit, walked at the
cold hours / Tameability 55% — meadow manners at frost
prices; it is the margin's easiest friendship and its
first lesson (the Pale Meadows are survived the way the
Meadow is loved: at ground level, in company). Drops:
shed white winter fur at the margin's thaw-line (the one
place either family thaws), **Pale Fur** (new item) — the
margin's own insulation, fifth door of the cold-weather
series and the first from OUTSIDE Frost proper. Hook:
every clash and kinship on the graph deserves one citizen
a new traveller can befriend at the border; the pale hare
is the north's.

**108. Grave Vine** — Rootwood x Bone (place name draft:
the Grave Roots). Flora x Ordinary. Shape: Plantiform;
modifiers: bone-climbing — it grows THROUGH the hybrid
country's skeletons, rooting in root-soil, flowering from
ribs and eye-sockets, and a leviathan in the Grave Roots
wears its spine in white flowers by the second year.
Locomotion: Stationary. Spectra (flora reading): Social
skeleton-lines — it follows the dead the way the Deck
Weaver follows bridges / Diet light, root-soil, and the
bones' slow weather (the Bonemoss's diet at vine scale,
with flowers) / Day-night: it blooms at dusk, bone-white
opening in the green dark, the hybrid country's one
reliable beauty mark / Territory one skeleton, then its
neighbor — it crosses between bones on root-bridges of
its own growing (the moss needed the ants' roads; the
vine builds its own, being Rootwood's child) / Cultivation:
startable on bone laid in root-soil — both parents'
conditions, or nothing (the roster's fussiest flora, and
proud) / Harvest: dusk-cut flowers, **Gravevine Flower**
(new item) — pale, cold-scented; a decoration with a
provenance, which builders will pay in trade long before
any recipe claims it. Hook: the Graveyard's garden had a
moss for its floor and ants for its crew; the hybrid
country's garden grows UP — the vine is what the two
families' grief looks like when it flowers.

**109. Ashpicker** — Ember x Bone (place name draft: the
Ashen Fields). Fauna x Ordinary. Shape: Winged (picker
bird plan); modifiers: ash-gray, ember-eyed. Locomotion:
Aerial, flyer-leaning + Walking. Spectra: industrious /
loose ash companies / mid / mid / Diet picker-scavenger —
it works the country where the Ashswimmer dives, taking
what the ash gives up: cooked seeds, surfaced rings,
the Drift's small dead (the swimmer hunts the ash from
below; the picker gleans it from above; the Ashen Drift
has a second citizen and the Ashen Fields their first) /
diurnal / Curiosity mid / Territory an ash-circuit /
Tameability 20%, provision-earned over days — the
Bonepicker's taming rule in its cousin country (the two
pickers are the roster's scavenger rhyme: provision a
picker, anywhere, and the picker keeps the account).
Drops: molted feathers where it roosts: **Ash Feather**
(new item) — kept DISTINCT from Grave Feather and Hawk
Feather as a matter of house law: three skies, three
feathers, no substitutions. Hook: a family pairing whose
whole ground is two kinds of remains produces, properly,
a professional.

**110. False Greeter** — Living x Precursor (the far
margin of the two strangest families). *The mimic the
biome program's seat note wondered about, placed where
it belongs: not in either family, but between them.*
Fauna x Forged. Shape: Plantiform — the Greeter's exact
silhouette, leaf-hands, hello-posture and all; modifier:
WRONG AT THE FEET — where the Greeter roots, the False
Greeter stands on forged root-feet, unrooted, finished
underneath like a made thing. Locomotion: Walking — it
can leave, which no Greeter can; the difference is the
diagnosis. Spectra: temperament a PERFECT hello — warm,
patient, exact (it learned the gesture by watching
across the border for a very long time; it performs the
valley's greeting the way the Spiral Moth wears the
garden's spiral: completely, and as a pattern) / Social
SOLO / Speed the Greeter's, plus one wrongness: it does
not sway when no wind blows / mid / Diet recorded as
UNCLEAR, in the template's own slot, with the design's
blessing: it accepts offerings the way the Greeter does,
and what it does with them has not been observed /
Day-night the valley's hours / Curiosity: it IS curiosity,
worn as a greeting / Territory the margin, walked / UNTAMABLE —
not dangerous, not tameable: the design records it as a
CITIZEN OF THE MARGIN, a neighbor-power like the Warden,
at flora scale: you may greet it, trade with it after its
fashion (it leaves garden-cuttings for valley-fruit, in
exact piles, at the border, overnight), and rely on its
hello being, every time, precisely a hello and nothing
else — which, after a hundred species of greetings that
might be grabs, is its own kind of uncanny. Drops: none.
Hook: the roster's hundredth-and-tenth is the first
species ABOUT the roster — a made thing that learned the
living family's first word by listening, and says it
flawlessly, and means it in a language of one. After the
Hearth Dog — the creature that means its welcome — the
margin keeps the one that performs it. Both are true
citizens. The difference is the game.

Roster designed total: 110. Hybrid cast 33 of 55
pairings. Part 3 opens with the count at three figures,
which was the order Adam gave: this IS BioForge, and Bio
is key.
