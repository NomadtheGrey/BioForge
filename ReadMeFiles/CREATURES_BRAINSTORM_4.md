## Seventeenth roster pass — the epic blanks

Adam's standing order (2026-10-15): fill in the blanks
for epic-large creatures at will. This pass completes
the epic set — every family and medium that lacked a
landscape-class citizen gets one, plus the HUGE tier
beneath them where a family had no giant at all.

**The epic distribution ruling** (Adam asked: noise-
generated, random, or what? — answered here, once, for
all epics, retroactive to the Leviathans and the
Whale): epics are NOT census and NOT noise scatter.
An epic is a UNIQUE INDIVIDUAL, seeded like geography:
exactly one per epic species per world (per far
region, for the migratory ones), placed by the world
seed deterministically — same seed, same giant, same
migration — the way zone rolls and ruin fates are
seeded, not the way herds scatter. Noise decides where
the herds are; the seed itself decides the giants,
once. An epic does not respawn: if one dies, the
world is permanently poorer, and the codex records
the date. They are discovered by SIGN before they are
ever seen — tracks, shed weather, the entourage's
behavior — because meeting one should feel like
finding a mountain that wasn't on the map, and the
map was right about everything else.

**183. Garden Colossus** — Precursor (the far garden
country). *Fauna x Forged* — **LANDSCAPE**, the fifth
use of the word. Shape: Biped at hill scale (the
Garden Warden's plan, written in mountains); modifier:
garden-backed — a spiral bed grows on its shoulders,
 tended by nothing, blooming in rows as it walks.
Locomotion: Walking — it MIGRATES between garden
sites across centuries, and it still GARDENS: its
route is a line of planted forests in spiral order,
each planted a human lifetime apart (the tenders'
god, or their first draft — the entry records both
readings and endorses neither). Spectra: abyssally
calm, the epic temperament / SOLO / a season's pace /
LANDSCAPE / Diet none recorded — it photosynthesizes
its gardens' surplus, or eats nothing, or eats years;
the slot is left UNCLEAR by design / Day-night: it
sleeps decades, standing, mistaken for a ruin with
trees / Curiosity none / Territory a route /
UNTAMABLE — geography that files paperwork in trees.
Tell, per the ruling: its SIGN is a forest in spiral
rows where no garden stands, younger than the ruins,
older than the zones. Hook: Precursor's scale topped
out at a warden; the colossus is the family's true
size — the gardens were always a giant's hobby.

**184. Valley Heart** — The Living. *Flora* x
Ordinary — **LANDSCAPE**, stationary: a single tree
at the scale of the country's heart, whose canopy is
a MICRO-BIOME (its own weather, its own citizens —
Guest Birds nest in it that have never touched the
valley's floor). Locomotion: Stationary, for
millennia. Spectra (flora reading): temperament the
valley's, AMPLIFIED — the Heart is where the
Welcoming/Hungry split is DECIDED, season by season,
by measures the entry leaves to the far future's
lore (recorded as the family's deepest open hook,
deliberately) / Social: it IS the society / Diet
light, the valley's water, and the valley's whole
regard / Day-night: its canopy keeps its own /
Cultivation never / Harvest: its fallen heart-leaves,
gathered at the rim: in a Welcoming year, medicine;
the entry does not finish the other sentence.
Taming's flora-form: a Welcoming Heart will grow a
BRANCH down to a long-kept homestead's roof — the
family's hug, at tree scale, across a generation.
Hook: the Living had mirrors at every scale except
the largest; the Heart is the mirror the whole
valley is reflected in.

**185. Mother Grove** — Fungal (the family's heart
country). *Fungus* x Ordinary — **LANDSCAPE by area**:
not one fruiting body but ONE ORGANISM — a single
mycelium miles across, of which the groves are
fingers. The Spore Stag walks ON it; the Hollow is
its dreaming (the entry offers that as codex flavor,
marked as such). Locomotion: Stationary, at the
speed of growth. Spectra (fungus reading): Social:
it is the family's whole society, underground /
Diet the country's entire fall, across centuries /
Day-night: it fruits in WAVES that cross the family
like weather — the Meltcap fields and Puffbell
plantings are its local clocks, synchronized by
being, literally, one body / Cultivation: everything
planted in its country is already its tenant /
Harvest: its year's fruiting is the family's harvest
festival that nobody organizes. Tell: in a Mother
Grove year, EVERY fungus in the family fruits on the
same three nights — a synchronization no one can
miss, and the pass's gift to the far calendar. Hook:
fauna got leviathans and flora got the Heart; the
fungus kingdom's epic was never going to be a
creature you meet — it's the ground being one
animal, patiently, everywhere.

**186. Sleeping Mountain** — Ember (the Ashen
Drift's far edge). Fauna x Ordinary — **LANDSCAPE**.
Shape: Quadruped at terrain scale; modifier:
ash-mantled — it has slept so long under the Drift
that maps draw it as a hill, and the Ashswimmer
dives in the ash of its flanks (the entry braids:
the swimmer's whole country is one animal's
bedding). Locomotion: Walking, in principle — it
moves perhaps once a century, at night, and the
Drift's geography is quietly revised; between moves
it BREATHES, and the breathing moves ash dunes in
rhythm (the Drift's second weather). Spectra:
abyssally calm past the epic standard — its calm has
geology in it / SOLO / a century's pace / LANDSCAPE /
Diet grazer, in its waking weeks, of whole ash-meadows
/ Day-night irrelevant to it / Curiosity none /
Territory where it lies / UNTAMABLE — geography,
asleep. Tell: the hill that breathes — a rhythm in
the ash that no wind keeps. Hook: Ember's epic is
its landscape confessing; the family's danger bench
hunts in the ash this citizen sheds.

**187. First Tree** — Rootwood. *Flora* x Ordinary —
**LANDSCAPE**, stationary: a single tree whose
canopy is a zone's ceiling, still standing while the
Rootfall's giant lies fallen beside its country (the
two are a pair in the family's memory: one fell, one
didn't, and the Deck Weavers' bridges are built in
the standing one's foothills — the entry records the
Rootfall's bear denning in the fallen sibling's
hollow within sight of the standing one's crown, and
leaves the image alone). Locomotion: Stationary.
Spectra (flora reading): Social: a country unto
itself — canopy sloths that never descend live whole
lives in it (the sloth entry's "never" gets its one
exception, here) / Diet light at national scale /
Day-night: its shade is a climate / Cultivation
never — Arch Trees are its children, plantable by
no one / Harvest: its fallen limbs are Heartwood's
elders, gathered rarely, at the family's richest
door. Tell: you are in its country when the light
comes through leaves at NOON as if through evening.
Hook: flora epics now stand in three families —
Heart, Grove, Tree — and each is a place, which is
what plants at this scale honestly are.

**188. Glass Behemoth** — Saltglass (the deep Flats).
Fauna x Crystalline — HUGE, the tier under LANDSCAPE,
kept distinct on purpose: it can be SEEN ACROSS, end
to end, on a clear day, which no true epic allows.
Shape: Quadruped; modifier: prism-bodied — its body
is a walking Prism Bed: light enters its flank and
exits sorted, so it travels inside a personal rainbow
weather (Prism Frogs bask in its refractions as it
passes; the Prism Cat hunts its shadow's edge —
the family's whole cast attends it). Locomotion:
Walking, in long seasonal crossings of the flats.
Spectra: calm / SOLO / slow / HUGE / Diet grazer of
salt flora and glass-country grit / diurnal — it
IS a daylight phenomenon / Curiosity none /
Territory a crossing / Tameability 0%. Tell: a
rainbow with no rain, moving against the wind, low
on the flats. Drops: shed plates of grown glass
along its route: **Behemoth Glass** (new item) —
the thickest natural saltglass in the world, window-
grade for the far builders. Hook: Saltglass's giant
is its beauty at scale — a weather system made of
the family's one substance, walking.

**189. Old Marrow** — Bone (the Marrow Flats).
Fauna x Ordinary — HUGE. Shape: Quadruped (tortoise
plan — the Boneback's grandmother, singular); modifier:
forest-shelled — bonemoss grows on her in succession
forests, and Skull Ants keep ORCHARDS of gravebloom
in her shell's hollows (she is a Graveyard that
walks, tended by the Graveyard's whole staff,
without leaving the Flats). Locomotion: Walking, at
a pace the ants' generations measure their calendars
by. Spectra: abyssally calm — the tortoise standard
at full size / SOLO, with staff / a season's pace,
barely / HUGE / Diet grazer of the Flats' pale growth /
Day-night: she sleeps through the cold snaps that
empty the fields / Curiosity low / Territory a flats
circuit / Tameability: the Boneback's rule grown
old — you cannot tame her, but camps that keep her
stopping-places clean are STOPPED AT, generation
after generation: follow, arriving at tortoise
speed, as a family tradition. Drops: none. Hook:
Bone's living giant is a citizen the death family's
upkeep-crew commutes to; between her and the Storm
Leviathan, the Graveyard's scale is bracketed by
the walking and the dead.

**190. Winter Mother** — Frost (the Stillwhite's

Walking, migration-paced. Spectra: calm, wary at

Day-night indifferent in the white / Curiosity low /
Territory the Stillwhite's deep circuit /
Tameability 0%. Tell: her tracks are ponds in

melt-pools, readable for a year. Drops: shed winter

door of the series, at the largest scale. Hook:
Frost's emblem is a stag and its danger is a pack;
its EPIC is a mother — the steady family's scale is
domestic, and the entry finds that correct.

**191. Dune Whale** — Saltglass x Storm (the

**192. Long Grazer** — Meadow (the deep plains).
Fauna x Ordinary — HUGE. Shape: Quadruped
(sauropod plan — the plains' second scale, at herd

Spectra: placid / HERDS — the social spectrum at

**193. Great Hermit** — Fungal (the Hollow's deep

Shape: Husk primary + Multiped. Locomotion: Walking,

Spectra: ancient-calm / SOLO, as a country is solo /
a decade's pace / HUGE / Diet the Hollow's whole

landmark / Curiosity: it TRADES, as the Hermit does —
Hollow Heart Spores at dynasty rates, for things

neighbor-power at full size. Tell: a chamber that

the Hollow's cast had a hermit; its depths keep the
hermit's ELDER — the pass's solemn comedy, kept

**194. Tempest Worm** — Storm (the Charged Flats'

Segmented (worm plan, at the scale the word starts

worm eats the lightning's delivery system, below).
Locomotion: Burrowing, storm-timed. Spectra:
appetite-calm / SOLO / front-speed underground /
HUGE / Diet fulgurite, strike-fused grit, deep
mineral / storm-locked to the family's whole
calendar / Curiosity none / Territory a flats'
deep field / Tameability 0%. Tell: before a front,
its tunnels HUM with gathered charge — the flats
sing a bass note the Zot Stars answer by turning
down — and the Static Crabs ground themselves and

in sky (Roc), storm (Leviathan), and now BELOW —
the family's whole column, like the water's, is
furnished.
