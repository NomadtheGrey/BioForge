## Twenty-fourth roster pass — Fungus Flood Three (24)

The kingdom's depth wave: materials the floods had
only touched (Gel, Vapor, Forged at fungus scale),
habits the casts had missed (night, deep, mirror,
steam, trail), and the wide-range fungi that walk —
at spore speed — between the families. With this
pass the fungus kingdom reaches ~86 designed, level
with the flora: the three kingdoms stand balanced
for the first time since the roster was counted.

**313. Lake Jelly** — *Fungus* x **Gel** — the
kingdom's second gel citizen (the great lakes'
deeps). A gel fungus fruiting on the Deep Reef's
structures, in black water (the reef builds the
city; the jelly lights its windows — faintly,
greenly, at depths where the Pale Eel hunts by a
light it didn't make). Stationary. Harvest: **Lake
Jelly** (item) — the deep's own gel, carried up in
sealed jars: the Gloom Gel's abyssal grade.

**314. Mist Cap** — *Fungus* x **Vapor** — the
kingdom's second vapor citizen (the Mirror
country's fog banks). A cap that fruits IN FOG and
makes more: its spore-release is a local thickening
of the mist (walk the Mirror country's banks at dawn
and the fog has orchards — caps at head height in
the murk, each in its own weather). Stationary.
Harvest: caps, unlisted — the fog's provision,
gathered by feel as much as sight.

**315. Tool Cap** — *Fungus* x **Forged** — the
kingdom's second forged citizen (the Garden). A
BRED utility cap: its flesh is a springy foam (the
gardeners grew it as padding — for gloves, for
cradles, for the Tender's grippers — and it still
fruits in the beds' tool-rows, cushion-shaped, in
graded sizes, three centuries after the last hand
it was grown for). Stationary. Harvest: **Tool
Foam** (new item) — grown padding, cut to shape:
the far crafters' cushion, with the Anvil Cap's
bracket as its workbench.

**316. Sail Cap** — *Fungus* x **Vapor** — the
kingdom's third vapor citizen (Skyfall Basin). A
cap that inflates a single SAIL of membrane and
rides the Basin's winds whole — fruiting body,
stalk, and a square inch of root-soil, traveling
(the Stormcap sends a cap; the sail cap goes
ITSELF, luggage and all — the kingdom's two
emigrations, the letter and the move). Stationary
where it lands. Harvest: never in flight; grounded
sails fruit a season where they fall, and the
Basin's farmers mark the landings like weather.

**317. Night Cap** — wide-range (Temperate). A cap
fruiting ONLY at night — up at dusk, down at dawn,
in meadow, marsh-edge, and grove alike (the Meltcap
keeps the fungal family's night in its own country;
the night cap is the night shift's wide-range
citizen, and moths of three families pollinate...
the entry corrects itself: fungi are not pollinated;
the moths merely attend, in numbers that suggest an
opinion). Stationary, nightly. Harvest: at midnight,
**Night Cap** (item) — the dark hours' provision,
picked by lantern across seven families.

**318. Storm Mold** — wide-range (Storm + Open). A
mold that blooms in the HOURS AFTER a front — gray-
green films on stone and stem, gone in two days (it
is the fronts' signature at ground level: where the
storm mold has bloomed, weather has been; surveyors
read it like a watermark). Stationary, briefly.
Harvest: none — its yield is the record.

**319. Dark Cap** — the surface's lightless places
(Underroot, bridge-undersides, husks, the Graveyard's
interiors). *Fungus* x Ordinary. A cap of the
shallow dark — everywhere the overworld keeps a
shadow with soil in it (the cave bestiary is parked
by Adam's order; the dark cap is the surface's own
citizen of the same idea, and the entry marks the
border respectfully: this one stops where the true
caves begin). Stationary. Harvest: **Dark Cap**
(item) — the shade's provision, glow-gilled faintly,
kin to the Gloom Cap in the next parish of dark.

**320. Husk Cap** — Bone (the Graveyard). *Fungus*
x Ordinary. A cap fruiting INSIDE SKULLS — in the
eye-sockets' shade, in the cranium's damp (the
Skull Hermit's tenancy application, in the other
kingdom: the hermit wears the skull in life; the
husk cap fruits in it after — the Graveyard's
housing passes between kingdoms without paperwork).
Stationary. Harvest: caps, unlisted — gathered
from unoccupied skulls only, per the ants' treaty,
which the entry copies verbatim in spirit.

**321. Shell Cap** — the great lakes' shores.
*Fungus* x Ordinary. A cap fruiting on reef-shell
and lakestone at the waterline (the Deep Reef's
shore embassy: the reef builds below, the shell
cap fruits above, and the lake's whole column —
leviathan to cap — is one address). Stationary.
Harvest: caps, **Shell Cap** (item) — lacustrine,
mild, the lakes' own course.

**322. Trail Cap** — Meadow x Storm (the Leviathan's
road). *Fungus* x Ordinary. A cap fruiting on the
Grass Leviathan's DUNG, in its trail, at heroic
scale — a leviathan's leavings are events, and its
fungus is to match: caps the size of cartwheels in
a line across the plains, fruiting a week behind
the migration (the road's own harvest festival,
unorganized, attended by everything — the entry
calls it the plains' moving market). Stationary.
Harvest: **Trail Cap** (item) — a single cap feeds
a camp; the plains' providence, at walking pace,
a week behind the weather that made it.

**323. Sweet Cap** — The Living. *Fungus* x
Ordinary. A honey-tasting cap of the valley groves
(the Greeting Cap bows in the hybrid country; the
sweet cap is the family's own table fungus).
Mirror, by TASTE, completing the family's set of
senses: in a Welcoming valley it is honey, exactly.
In a Hungry valley the same cap is honey for the
first bite — the entry records the custom of the
one-bite rule in the valley's kitchens and declines
to elaborate the second. Stationary. Harvest:
**Sweet Cap** (item) — with the one-bite rule
printed on the codex page in the old hand.

**324. Great Puff** — Meadow (the deep plains).
*Fungus* x Ordinary. A puffball at SHEEP SIZE — the
flood's giant, a white dome in the grass that the
Long Grazers step around (when it puffs, a meadow
holds its breath: the spore cloud is a small
weather, visible from the next zone; children are
forbidden, in every plains family, and the entry
records the forbidding as universal and futile).
Stationary. Harvest: young, **Great Puff** (item)
— a feast fungus, cut in steaks: the plains'
celebration course.

**325. Ink Cap** — Precursor (the ruins). *Fungus*
x Ordinary. A cap that MELTS into black ink at
maturity (Earth's inkcaps, under the familiar
license — the chroniclers' harvest: they gather
the melt in pots, and the Glyph Cap's one-day
sentences are copied across the far country IN
THIS FUNGUS — the kingdom writes its script and
then provides the ink: two citizens, one library).
Stationary. Harvest: the melt: **Ink** (new item)
— the far country's writing fluid, grown in its
libraries' ruins.

**326. Rust Cap** — Ember x Storm (the Iron Ashes).
*Fungus* x Ordinary. A cap of iron-red flesh
fruiting on the Ashes' slag (the Iron Cap takes the
country's metal as sheen; the rust cap takes it as
FLESH — two fungi divide the Ashes between surface
and substance, as the graph's citizens do).
Stationary. Harvest: **Rust Cap** (item) — strong,
metallic, the smelters' course, portion-noted like
its cousin.

**327. Snow Shelf** — Frost x Rootwood (the Frozen
Forest). *Fungus* x Ordinary. A bracket of the
frozen forest's trunks, shelving under rime (the
Rime Owl's hunting perches include its shelves —
the owl stands on the fungus to watch the voles:
three kingdoms in one ambush, and the entry files
the shelf as the forest's furniture with pride).
Stationary. Harvest: fallen shelves, unlisted —
the fallen-only custom, in its third parish.

**328. Sump Cap** — Wetland x Fungal (the sump
pools). *Fungus* x Ordinary. A glow-gilled cap
fruiting on the Poolcap's raft-edges and the pools'
own logs (the Sumpfish tends the pool; the sump cap
lights its rim — the fish's lantern and the pool's
boundary in one citizen). Stationary. Harvest:
caps, unlisted — the pools' fresh course, gathered
by their own light.

**329. Grave Puff** — Bone x Fungal (the charnel
country). *Fungus* x Ordinary. A puffball of the
charnel groves, gray and deliberate (the Charnel
Bloom impersonates a flower in this country; the
grave puff impersonates nothing — it is the
country's honest fungus, and its puff, when it
comes, is the color of the Bloom's lie dissolving:
gray, final, unglamorous, true). Stationary.
Harvest: young puffs, **Grave Puff** (item) — the
charnel country's provision, plain as its name.

**330. Salt Mold** — wide-range (Saltglass + Open).
*Fungus* x Ordinary. A crystalline-edged mold
filming the crust after brine runs (where the Salt
Jelly takes the cracks, the salt mold takes the
SURFACE — the flats' cross-section gains a film:
mold above, jelly in the crack, scorpion below,
and the Pan Lizard presiding as a statue over all).
Stationary. Harvest: none — it armors the crust
against wind; the flats' skin is not a crop.

**331. Ash Mold** — wide-range (Ember). *Fungus* x
Ordinary. The Ember country's general mold — on ash
wood, on old burns, on the Drift's leavings (the
Drift Mold is the variant's specialist; the ash
mold is the family's citizen, first after every
fire and last before every bloom: the Flame Lily
opens the calendar and the ash mold closes the burn
— between them, the whole fire year is fungal at
both ends and floral in the middle). Stationary.
Harvest: none — its yield is succession itself.

**332. Nest Cap** — Rootwood (the crowns).
*Fungus* x Ordinary. A broad, flat cap used by
canopy birds as a NEST BASE — they line it, lay in
it, and the cap fruits around the nestlings (the
Canopy Cap is eaten by sloths in passing; the nest
cap is INHABITED — Rootwood's fungus as architecture,
at bird scale, with the Deck Weaver's bridges as
its grown cousins in the other kingdom). Stationary.
Harvest: none while nested, ever; abandoned nests'
caps are timber-felt, unlisted, fallen-only by the
forest's whole custom.

**333. Mirror Cap** — Wetland (the Mirror Marsh).
*Fungus* x Ordinary. A cap whose upper surface is
a MIRROR — still, dark, reflective (the Mirror
Jelly floats the pools as the country's gel; the
mirror cap is its fungal citizen: pools of fungus
along the marsh paths, reflecting a sky a half-
second late, in keeping with the country's law —
travelers check their faces in it and are advised,
by the codex's driest line, to wave twice).
Stationary. Harvest: the cap is bitter, unlisted;
its yield is the reflection, and the directions.

**334. Sun Cap** — Meadow x Saltglass (the Pan
Meadows). *Fungus* x Ordinary. A cap that opens at
NOON and closes by two (the Noonflower keeps the
meadow's hour in the other kingdom; the sun cap
keeps the PAN's — between them, the hybrid country's
midday is announced twice, in flower and fungus,
and the Pan Piper plays to both). Stationary,
daily. Harvest: at noon, **Sun Cap** (item) — the
pans' brief course, gathered at a run.

**335. Frost Mold** — wide-range (Frost + Cold).
*Fungus* x Ordinary. A mold lining the SUBNIVEAN
world — the Frost Vole's tunnels are furred with it
(it eats the tunnels' damp and gives back a faint
warmth of decay: the vole's under-snow winter is
heated, in part, by this mold — the Cold Range's
smallest citizen is its furnace, and the vole's
whole civilization runs on it). Stationary.
Harvest: none — the vole's furnace is not gathered;
it is inherited, tunnel by tunnel.

**336. Steam Cap** — Ember x Frost (the clash
margins). *Fungus* x Ordinary. A cap fruiting on
the Steam Eel's channel banks, in the vapor where
ice meets near-boiling (the eel's country has a
fungus at last: it fruits in the steam, gills
dripping, on the warm bank only — the clash margin
is one channel wide, and its whole citizenry is an
eel, a cap, and the weather they share). Stationary.
Harvest: **Steam Cap** (item) — scalded-clean,
eaten at the bank, in the steam, by travelers who
have learned the margin's one address for dinner.

Fungus Flood Three closes the kingdom's floods:
~86 fungi designed, level with the flora, the
three kingdoms balanced. Roster designed total:
336. What remains in the standing order is the
wide-range FAUNA wave (the Far Bat first), and
then the roster's open edges are events, not
backlog: the Living's build seats, and the caves
at Adam's word.

## Twenty-fifth roster pass — Wide-Range Wave Two (12)

The semi-ubiquitous layer's second wave, named back
in pass 11 by its gaps: the cold, warm, and far
ranges ran thin (Frost and Ember held two each, the
far families one). Twelve thickeners, the Far Bat
first as promised. With this pass, the layer stands
at 24 and the ranges stand even — and the DESIGN
BACKLOG named across these passes stands empty:
what remains after this is events, not queue.

**337. Far Bat** — the Far Range (Storm, Precursor,
the Living, deep Fungal). Fauna x Ordinary. Shape:
Winged (bat plan); modifier: ruin-roosting — it
sleeps in ruins, stormcatcher lees, and the valley's
 high eaves, and commutes between them at dusk (it
eats Mote Swarms on the wing — the swarm is its
pasture — and where a far homestead keeps a high,
dark eave, a far bat takes the tenancy within a
season: the far country's swallow). Locomotion:
Aerial, flyer-leaning. Spectra: serene / colonial
at roosts, solitary hunting / quick / small / Diet
aerial plankton of the far dusk — motes, gnats,
driftmoth scales taken as seasoning / nocturnal /
Curiosity low / Territory a roost-circuit / Tameability
30% — roost-loyal like the pickers: keep the eave,
keep the bat. Drops: guano at old roosts — compost's
far grade, unlisted, collected with respect.
Hook: the far families shared one citizen (the
driftmoth); the bat is their second, and the first
that comes HOME to roost — the Far Range, domesticated
at the edges.

**338. Winter Wren** — the Cold Range (Frost,
Saltglass, Bone, Storm's heights). Fauna x Ordinary.
Shape: Winged (wren plan, round as a snowball);
modifier: under-snow singer — it sings from BENEATH
the snowpack's edge, in the vole's own airspace,
and its song comes out of the snowfield like the
field is humming (the Stillwhite's smallest voice;
Frost Voles navigate by it in the dark under
everything — the cress chimneys mark the light,
the wren marks the way). Locomotion: Aerial,
flyer-leaning + Hopping. Spectra: indomitable /
loose cold companies / quick / tiny / Diet snow-
fleas, cress seeds, the cold country's crumbs /
diurnal / Curiosity high / Territory a snowfield's
edge / Tameability 50% — it will take suet-analog
from a mitten, and the entry prices the mitten as
essential equipment. Drops: none. Hook: the Cold
Range thickens at its smallest scale first, which
the entry insists is how cold countries work.

**339. Thaw Mouse** — the Cold Range's EDGE
(Saltglass's Thaw, Frost's margins, the early
Meadow) — and the entry states its joke plainly,
because the joke is the layer's founding law: mice
don't live in the arctic — but a mouse lives at the
arctic's DOOR. Fauna x Ordinary. Shape: Quadruped
(mouse plan); modifier: melt-timed — its year is
the thaw's: it wakes with the Thawmole, breeds in
the melt weeks, and is gone under the snowline
before the deep cold returns (the Burrow Mouse
holds the everywhere seat by the game's old census;
the thaw mouse is the honest version of the idea —
a mouse whose range RESPECTS the cold). Locomotion:
Walking + Burrowing in thaw-soft ground. Spectra:
busy / thaw companies / quick / small / Diet melt-
seed and thaw-pool life / diurnal / Curiosity total /
Territory the melt line, wherever it currently is —
a territory that MOVES, the roster's only migrating
border / Tameability 60%, the family standard.
Drops: none. Hook: Adam's sentence, given a citizen:
the semi-ubiquitous layer works because its ranges
have edges, and this mouse is the edge, walking.

**340. Dust Lizard** — the Warm + Open ranges
(Ember's margins, Saltglass, Bone's dry fields,
Meadow's dry side). Fauna x Ordinary. Shape:
Quadruped (lizard plan); modifier: dust-running —
it runs ON the dust's surface at speed, feet
slapping, never breaking through the crust's skin
(the Sun Lizard basks in the warm families; the
dust lizard RUNS the dry ones — two lizards divide
the warm world by verb). Locomotion: Walking,
burst-fast. Spectra: skittish / loose dry companies /
the lizard budget spent all at once / small / Diet
dry-country insects / diurnal / Curiosity mid /
Territory a dust flat / Tameability 35%. Drops:
none. Hook: the warm ranges' second lizard makes
the layer's point in small — coverage is not one
species per range but a CAST per range.

**341. Cinder Finch** — the Warm Range (Ember,
Meadow's warm side, Storm's lowlands). Fauna x
Ordinary. Shape: Winged (finch plan); modifier:
ash-billed — it cracks ember-country seeds no other
small bill attempts, and its throat feathers are
singed decoratively by its diet (the Ash Lark
dust-bathes over the same four families; the finch
EATS there — bather and diner, the warm ranges'
two small birds divide the country by appetite).
Locomotion: Aerial, flyer-leaning + Hopping.
Spectra: cheerful / finch companies / quick / small /
Diet hard warm seed / diurnal / Curiosity high /
Territory a seed-ground / Tameability 55% — the
feeder standard; it learns a kept feeder in a day
and brings the company in a week. Drops: none.
Hook: the Snow Finch holds the Cold Range's feeder;
the cinder finch holds the Warm's — the layer's
bookends, at two temperatures.

**342. Storm Thrush** — Temperate + Storm ranges
(Meadow, Fungal, Rootwood, Storm's edges). Fauna x
Ordinary. Shape: Winged (thrush plan); modifier:
front-singing — it sings BEFORE fronts, in the
pressure-drop stillness, louder as the weather
gathers (the flats have reeds and grass for the
zot; the settled country has the thrush for the
storm — a song at supper means a front by morning,
and homesteads across four families close their
shutters by it). Locomotion: Aerial, flyer-leaning.
Spectra: bold-voiced / loose companies / quick /
small / Diet ground insects, grove fruit / dawn-
dusk, and the pre-front hour / Curiosity mid /
Territory a singing grove / Tameability 40%.
Drops: none. Hook: the wide-range layer is also a
communications network; the thrush is its weather
service for the countries without lightning.

**343. Fen Finch** — the Wet Range (Wetland,
Rootwood's wet side, Meadow's marshes, Fungal's
damp). Fauna x Ordinary. Shape: Winged (finch
plan); modifier: reed-footed — it walks the reed
mats and lily edges like a small citizen of the
surface (the Reedbird owns the stands' voice; the
fen finch owns their FLOOR — it eats what falls
from the nests above, which the entry calls the
wet country's tidiest arrangement). Locomotion:
Aerial, flyer-leaning + Walking on mats. Spectra:
busy / fen companies / quick / small / Diet reed
seed, fallen insects / diurnal / Curiosity high /
Territory a reed mat / Tameability 50%. Drops:
none. Hook: the Wet Range's cast stood at five;
the finch makes six and gives its ponds a floor-
walker between the skater and the turtle.

**344. Plain Vole** — the Open Range (Meadow, Bone,
Saltglass, Storm's flats). Fauna x Ordinary. Shape:
Quadruped (vole plan); modifier: sky-watched — it
lives its whole life under open sky and reads it
professionally (a lifetime of Plainswifts and Dust
Hawks makes a philosopher: the plain vole freezes
at shadows in a taxonomy of which shadow is death —
the roster's most educated freeze). Locomotion:
Walking + Burrowing in open ground. Spectra: wary /
flat warrens / still-then-scurry / small / Diet dry
seed and root / crepuscular / Curiosity low /
Territory a warren under the sky / Tameability 35%
— its cousin under the snow is bolder; the sky
teaches otherwise. Drops: none. Hook: two voles
now bracket the layer — one under snow, one under
sky — and the open country's small life has its
citizen.

**345. Moon Moth** — Temperate + Warm ranges
(Meadow, Ember's margins, Storm's lowlands, the
Living's edge, Fungal's clearings). Fauna x
Ordinary. Shape: Winged (great pale moth plan);
modifier: candle-drawn — it is the Night Candle's
pollinator, NAMED AT LAST: the candle lays a scent-
road and the moon moth flies it like a river (the
entry closes the flood's open loop deliberately —
flora and fauna are one document in the field).
Locomotion: Aerial, glider-leaning, nocturnal.
Spectra: serene / SOLO / drifting / LARGE for a
moth — the Driftmoth's mild-country cousin, in size
and in temperament / Diet night blooms, candle
first / NOCTURNAL absolute / Curiosity scent-led /
Territory a night's candle-road / Tameability 0% —
it does not land near lights it didn't choose.
Drops: shed scales on the candle blooms, unlisted.
Hook: the night shift's wide-range citizen — five
families' worth of dark, patrolled by one pale
moth, on business the flowers arranged.

**346. Ruin Sparrow** — the ruin-bearing families
(Precursor first, and wherever old places are:
Bone's Graveyard stones, Rootwood's Rootfall,
Ember's old burns). Fauna x Ordinary. Shape: Winged
(sparrow plan); modifier: masonry-nesting — it
nests in WALLS: in ruin joints, in the Graveyard's
stacked bones, in any kept stone with a sparrow-
sized mercy in it (the Hedge Sparrow holds the
settled country's hedges; the ruin sparrow holds
its RUINS — the layer's two sparrows divide the
world into the kept and the left, and both are
inhabited). Locomotion: Aerial, flyer-leaning +
Hopping. Spectra: bold / masonry colonies / quick /
small / Diet ruin insects, glyph-cap spores taken
as seasoning / diurnal / Curiosity total — it
investigates camps, tools, and open codexes /
Territory a wall / Tameability 60% — the crumb
economy, in ruins as at home. Drops: none. Hook:
its range is drawn by HISTORY, not climate — the
layer's first citizen whose map is the past.

**347. Sleet Frog** — the Cold + Wet ranges
(Frost's margins, Wetland's cold side, Saltglass's
thaw pools). Fauna x Ordinary. Shape: Quadruped
(frog plan); modifier: ice-tolerant — it sings in
thaw pools with ice still in them, the first voice
of the cold country's spring (the Hello Frog greets
in the valley's marshes; the sleet frog greets the
THAW — its chorus from a half-frozen pool is the
Cold Range's whole promise, made in one wet voice).
Locomotion: Hopping + Swimming. Spectra: cheerful
in weather that empties ponds / thaw choruses /
burst / small / Diet thaw-pool life / dusk /
Curiosity vocal / Territory one cold pool /
Tameability 45% — reciprocal calling works in the
cold too, slower. Drops: none. Hook: the Pond
Frog's unbuilt chair from the first wave is filled
in the cold instead — the layer decided the
temperate ponds had citizens enough, and the thaw
had none.

**348. Field Crow** — Temperate + Open + Wet
ranges (Meadow, Rootwood, Fungal, Wetland, Bone's
edges, Saltglass's edges). Fauna x Ordinary. Shape:
Winged (crow plan, honestly); modifier: trading —
the Storm Crow runs the far country's barter at
legendary scale; the field crow runs the SETTLED
country's, at pocket scale: buttons, bright wire,
one memorable spoon (its Crow Gift category is the
same, the stakes domestic — the entry establishes
the genus: crows trade, everywhere crows are; the
families only set the prices). Locomotion: Aerial,
flyer-leaning + Walking with intent. Spectra:
bold / loose companies with long memories / quick /
mid / Diet omnivore of the settled world /
diurnal / Curiosity TOTAL — the temperate country's
most thorough investigator / Territory a circuit
of farms, camps, and middens / Tameability 35%,
by barter over a season — it does not bond; it
CONTRACTS, and keeps its side. Drops: Crow Gifts.
Hook: wave two closes with the layer's thesis in
one bird: a species is wide-range not because it is
everywhere, but because everywhere it goes, it is
ITSELF — memory, trade, and opinions included.

The semi-ubiquitous layer stands complete at 24 —
two waves, six ranges, every family holding 4+ wide-

The design backlog is EMPTY. What remains is

## Twenty-sixth roster pass — The Living, seated (10)

The Living is BUILT (the biome program, 2026-10-15),
and the build named its seats, as every build has.
The facts this pass honors, from their doc: the
family's variants ARE its two faces — *Welcoming
Valley* (min 4, rare) and *Hungry Valley* (min 5,
rare) — so the variant seats are one creature for
each temperament. Their flora stands planted:
DUSKGRASS (black-violet, the family's ground) and
the HEARTBLOOM — one per zone at its seeded heart
point, harvesting to HEART SAP, double when fed,
and accepting fertilizer as a MEAL that satiates a
hungry valley. Their note stands over the whole
pass: "the valley itself is the organism — its
signature 'creature' may be the valley entire."
Our Greeter and the Valley Heart answered that at
family scale; this pass seats the two faces and the
Heartbloom's citizenry.

**349. Gladhand** — Welcoming Valley ONLY (the
face's one-off; it does not exist in Hungry valleys,
and its absence at a valley's edge is itself data).
Fauna x Ordinary. Shape: Plantiform (the Greeter's
kin at walking scale); modifier: many-handed — a
dozen leaf-hands along its arms, all of them
offered. Locomotion: Walking. Its profession is
USHER: it meets visitors at the valley's edge,
takes a hand (a gauntlet serves; it holds gently
and lets go the instant the hold is resisted — the
family's covenant, kept in miniature), and walks
you by the pleasant route to the Heartbloom.
