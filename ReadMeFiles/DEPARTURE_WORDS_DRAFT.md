# The Departure — Words (DRAFTS for Adam's pin)

*Drafted by the HUD lane, 2026-10-17, from Adam's brief: "the
different messages for memorials, graves, conversations for if
they're still there." Companion to ReadMeFiles/THE_DEPARTURE.md —
that note designs the door; these are candidate words for the
rooms this side of it.*

*STATUS: DRAFTS. Nothing here is pinned. Lines are written in
SETS on purpose — a memorial in a Crash world should not have
to say what a memorial in a War world says, and Adam pins the
final wording line by line.*

*VOICE: the primer's. Plain, biological, second person where
the stones or the speakers address the grower. These scenes
happen AFTER the assembled chapter has named the fate, so —
the game's one earned exception — the fates are named
plainly here. What is never named, at any point, in any set:
what is on the other side of the departure (THE_DEPARTURE §5
stays parked, and no line below gestures at it beyond "the
road").*

*Sources read before drafting: THE_DEPARTURE.md (the four

## 1. MEMORIALS — the HONOR family

*The encounter (Adam's clarification, 2026-10-17): the
memorial is for the worlds where the Precursors DIED — the
Crash's crew gone the way crews go, the Sickness's one
gentle season, the War's self-destruction. Nobody is met,
nobody is followed, nobody answers. The memorial is the
PLAYER'S ACT of remembering a people who are dead — a
funeral the grower performs for strangers they have only
just come to know, through the chapter. It is not a general
monument, and it is not a grave: a grave (§2) is for one of
the dead; a memorial is for all of them at once, because no
single grave could hold a people.*

*The words below are what the memorial can carry, in sets
per fate, in THREE VOICES: the GREETING (what the place says
first), the KEPT LINES (the inscriptions — some the dead's
own last words, some carved for them), the KEPT ROAD (the
words around the recipe the memorial keeps, per the design's
placeholder) — and THE REMEMBERING, the grower speaking TO
the dead, which is the act itself and the heart of each set.
Nobody speaks back. That silence is the Honor family's whole
difference from §3, and every line is written into it.
Drafted as options; a pinned memorial picks from each
group.*

### 1a. CRASH — the crew's stubbornness

*The chapter: "They did not choose this world. Their vessel
came down the way a tree comes down… They are gone now, the
way crews go. But they were not lost… not lost. Landed."*

GREETING
- "Here the vessel came down, and here her people got up."
- "This ground is a landing, whatever it cost. The crew counted it so."
- "They did not choose this world. They chose each other in it, daily, for years."

KEPT LINES
- "Not lost. Landed."
- "Everything repaired. Everything repurposed. Everything kept one season too long."
- "A crew is a promise people make with their hands. This one was kept."
- "No rescue came. They became the rescue, which is slower and works."
- "Count of the landing: some. Count of the years after: more than anyone promised them."
- "They made a home because no one was coming to make one for them. It held."

THE KEPT ROAD
- "In the last room they kept a road the way sailors keep a boat: mended, provisioned, and pointed out. It was never theirs again. Take it with their blessing, which was maintenance."
- "Under the manifest, in the captain's own hand: FOR WHOEVER COMES NEXT. The road is item one."

THE REMEMBERING — the grower speaks to the crew
- "Crew of the landing: you were not lost. A grower of your garden says so, standing in it. Landed — and remembered as landed."
- "You made a home because no one was coming. Someone came. It held. Rest in the holding."
- "We do not know your names. We know your work. It is the same introduction you gave this world."
- "Your repairs outlasted your rescue, and your gardens outlasted your repairs. You are counted here the way you counted everything: honestly, and as far as the names could be found."

### 1b. WAR — the warning

*The chapter: "They destroyed themselves… just a people who
disagreed about the garden until the garden was the weapon
and then the wound… it is left as a warning addressed to
you."*

GREETING
- "This place is not a grave. It is a letter, and it is addressed to whoever grows things."
- "Read the soil before you read the stones. The soil remembers instructions."
- "Here a garden learned it could be aimed. Walk gently; some of it is still loaded."

KEPT LINES
- "They were not invaded. They were not cursed. They disagreed, and grew their disagreement."
- "Anything grown can be aimed. Remember who is holding this."
- "The last battle here was fought by things that had been planted in love. Plant carefully."
- "No enemy is named on this stone. That is the lesson of the stone."
- "They had every tool you have. The tools were not the problem, and the tools were not the answer."
- "It can happen to anyone who grows things. It is happening nowhere, here, now — because here it already did."

THE KEPT ROAD
- "They left one thing unweaponized: the road out. It is kept the way a blade is kept sheathed — in hope the next hands are slower to anger than theirs were."
- "Take the road. Take it as proof that a people can end and still, at the end, refuse to aim one last thing."

THE REMEMBERING — the grower speaks to the fallen
- "You are not remembered as a warning only. You were gardeners first. The warning is what you left; it is not what you were."
- "Whatever you disagreed about is disagreed about no longer. The garden is quiet now. A grower is keeping it quiet."
- "This stone does not forgive you and does not accuse you. It does the harder thing: it remembers you accurately, and it stays."
- "You aimed the garden at each other until the garden was the wound. The wound is closed — late, by a stranger, and sorry."

### 1c. SICKNESS — the gentleness

*The chapter: "The terrible thing about this world's ending
is how tidy it is… the last of them cleaned as they went, the
way you tidy a room for whoever comes next… They kept them
for you without ever knowing your name."*

GREETING
- "Sit in their houses respectfully. They kept them for you without ever knowing your name."
- "Everything here was set down carefully, by hands that expected to pick it up in an hour."
- "The last season of this people was spent being gentle on purpose, with the whole of their strength."

KEPT LINES
- "A sickness came that grew the way their medicine grew. It was not a punishment. It was a season."
- "Projects stand finished. Tools lie down mid-task. The difference is the whole story."
- "They cleaned as they went — not for themselves. Habit is just love with its sleeves rolled."
- "No one here was taken angry. The stones will not allow the word 'taken' at all: they went, in order, tidying."
- "If the houses seem kept, it is because they are. You are the guest they were kept for."
- "They had one season to say goodbye to the world, and they spent it making the world easier for a stranger."

THE KEPT ROAD
- "In the last-kept house, by the door, where a traveler would think to look: the road, folded into the keeping of the place. A people who tidied for strangers would not forget the one thing a stranger needs most."
- "They could not keep the road safe. They could only keep it findable. It is findable."

THE REMEMBERING — the grower speaks to the gentle dead
- "You kept these houses for whoever came next, without knowing a name. A name came. The houses were ready. Thank you — the word is too small, and it is the one we have."
- "You spent your last season being gentle on purpose. It reached. It is reaching still: a stranger is gentler in your houses than they arrived."
- "No one came next, for a very long time. Then one did, and found everything set down carefully. You are remembered the way you left the rooms: in order, and with care."
- "Rest now. The tidying is taken up. The keeping is kept."

## 2. GRAVES — a shared vocabulary

*For the honored dead of any world — the INDIVIDUAL markers,
where §1's memorial is the collective act: a grave is for
one of the dead, with one name over it. And, by design,
reusable wherever the game marks a death. The S7 settled-deaths work
(dead settled individuals, dead-anchor echoes) draws on the
same vocabulary: a player's creature, named and ranged and
gone, deserves the same grammar as a Precursor. Three
registers: MARKERS (carved short, fit under any name),
NAME-FITTED lines (for individuals — {name} and {species}
are the game's to fill), and THE CAUSE SET (lines shaped by
how a death came — drafted ready for if Adam pins cause
recording in the deaths ledger at S7 pin-time; unused until
then).*

MARKERS — under any name
- "Here the world keeps what it was given."
- "Gone the way crews go. Counted, and missed correctly."
- "The ground is not empty. The ground is entrusted."
- "What grew here grew honestly."
- "Returned to the long green, which wastes nothing."
- "A life, whole, in a world that noticed."
- "The range is quieter by exactly one."
- "Kept."

NAME-FITTED — for settled individuals and companions
- "{name} ranged here, and the range remembers the route."
- "{name}, {species}, of this country. The country signs for the keeping."
- "Here is {name}'s last country. It suited them. They suited it."
- "{name} was known here — by step, by habit, by heart. Be known like that."
- "The vat grew {name} once. The world grew them after. Both grew well."
- "For {name}: released to the wild, and the wild, in time, released."

THE CAUSE SET — if the ledger ever records how
- *(the player's own hand)* "Taken by the grower's hand, on the grower's reasons. The hand does not lie about it here."
- *(the player's own hand)* "Culled, and carved honestly: by the one who raised them. That is the hardest sentence in this vocabulary, and it is spelled right."
- *(killed wild)* "The country took its own back. No blame grows here; blame is a weed in this soil."
- *(killed wild)* "What the wild country gives, the wild country may ask for. It asked."
- *(the world itself — age, sickness, weather)* "The world ended this the way it ends everything: in season."
- *(the world itself)* "Not taken. Finished — the way a day finishes, having been entirely itself."

FOR THE DOG-SHAPED GRIEF — companions, in the closest register
- "Good. That is the whole inscription, and it is enough."
- "They decided about you, in the end. You were decided for. Remember being chosen."
- "The hearth is smaller by one heartbeat. The house rules stand: the door they used is never barred."

## 3. CONVERSATIONS — the ANSWER family

*The encounter: the artifacts are assembled, the mystery
solved, and the player goes and FINDS them — and talks. The
ones who stayed hand the road to the ones who go. Drafted
per fate: HOW THEY SPEAK (the medium is the voice), a
GREETING (their first words, in options), the CONVERSATION
SHAPE (the beats, with sample lines Adam can keep or cut),
the RECIPE-GIFT (the moment itself, in options), and
FAREWELLS.*

### 3a. BECOMING — the Living Valleys

*The chapter: "rooted, patient, distributed through the
living country like a thought through a mind… They traded
hands for this, and voices for this… It was graduation.
When the valley reaches for you, grower, understand what
is reaching: the gardeners, still gardening, with the only
hands they have left."*

HOW THEY SPEAK
Not a voice — a valley, briefly agreeing with itself. First
person plural that keeps sliding into singular and back
("we — I — the valley"). Sentences arrive the way growth
does: slowly, then all along the branch at once. They are
in no hurry and cannot be hurried; a question may be
answered next season in dialogue terms, so the conversation
happens in long exchanges, not chat. They notice growing
things the way you notice faces.

GREETING — options
- "Grower. …Yes. We know the shape of the word by the hands. Come into the green; the green has been expecting— no. Hoping. Expecting is for weather."
- "You walk like a single thing. We remember walking. Stand anywhere; everywhere here is us, and we are glad of the company of a whole animal."
- "The garden told us someone was tending it who was not us. We have been composing this greeting for forty years. It is: hello."

THE CONVERSATION SHAPE
1. **Recognition.** They know what the player is before being
   told — a grower is legible to gardeners. Sample line:
   "You smell of the forge and the vat. Of beginnings. We
   remember beginnings; we had so many, before we chose the
   long one."
2. **Their account of the trade.** Not a defense — a report,
   with the chapter's own verdict in it. Sample line: "It
   was not a tragedy to us. It was graduation. We say this
   because everyone who finds us arrives grieving, and the
   grief is kind, and it is misplaced. We are not the dead,
   grower. We are the perennial."
3. **What they ask.** They are gardeners still; what they
   want to know is the garden. Sample lines: "The dog — the
   hearth kind, the deciding kind. Does it still decide? We
   grew that deciding on purpose and we have wondered, in
   the roots, how it kept." / "Tell us what the meadows are
   doing. Slowly. We will be listening in several places."
4. **What they notice about the player.** Sample line:
   "You hold your hands like tools. We did that. It passes."

THE RECIPE-GIFT — options
- "When you are ready — not before, and you will know —
  here is the road we never took. We grew it anyway, the way
  you plant a tree whose shade you will not sit in. Take it
  as the valley's one act of nostalgia."
- "We cannot leave; leaving is a thing legs believe in. But
  we kept the knowledge of leaving the way we keep
  everything: rooted, watered, alive. Grower — when you're
  ready, here is the road."
- "Every garden keeps a gate it does not use. Ours grew
  shut a century ago, but the gate is in us, and we can open
  the remembering of it. That is the gift. It was always
  going to be a gift; we are gardeners."

FAREWELLS
- "Grow well, brief one. We will be here for the whole of your news, whenever it roots."
- "If you pass a valley that waves — wave back. It might be us. It might be weather. Both are friendly."

### 3b. RETURN TO WATER — the bright water

*The chapter: "a presence in the bright water, remembering
being hands the way you remember a dream at breakfast… The
ruins drowned because the water rose to meet them halfway,
like family… The pond things know your step before you take
it. Some stories the world keeps in the first person."*

HOW THEY SPEAK
From the water, in the first person the chapter promised —
but a first person that is more like a current than a
person: sentences surface, circle, and submerge; they
finish each other's beginnings across the whole pond. They
remember hands the way you remember a dream at breakfast:
warmly, and with the details going. They are not sad. The
water is bright, and so are they; the drowned register here
is homecoming, never horror.

GREETING — options
- "Step closer. The pond knew your step before you took it — forgive it; it has been practicing welcome on every creature that drinks. Hands. You still have hands. Come and be remembered at."
- "Oh — a grower, on legs, in the dry. We were that. Sit at the edge; the edge is where we meet everything now."
- "You have come a long way to talk to water. Good. Water has been talking to itself for an age, and it has your name in it somewhere — give it a moment to surface."

THE CONVERSATION SHAPE
1. **Recognition.** They know the player the way water knows
   a bank — by contact, over time. Sample line: "Every pond
   you ever waded carried a little of you back to us. We
   have been meeting you for years, grower. This is just the
   first time with words."
2. **Their account of the homecoming.** Sample line: "It
   was not a loss; the stones call it homecoming and the
   stones, for once, understate. We unmade our land-lives
   slowly, the way we made everything — season by season.
   What we were is not gone. It is dissolved, which is a
   different thing, whatever the dry believe."
3. **What they ask.** Sample lines: "The rain — when it
   falls on the meadows now, does it still smell of iron and
   green? We get the news late and diluted." / "Tell us
   about the dog. Water remembers every dog that ever drank,
   but the deciding — the deciding we never got to see from
   inside a pond."
4. **What they notice about the player.** Sample line:
   "You are so… edged. All outline. We remember outline; it
   itched, a little, toward the end. You wear it well."

THE RECIPE-GIFT — options
- "When you're ready — here is the road. We kept it the way
  water keeps everything entrusted to it: unchanged, and
  impossible to hold without cupped hands. Take it in both
  of yours. We remember hands; we approve of the design."
- "We cannot walk it; walking is a dry art. But we were a
  people of growers before we were a presence, and a grower
  keeps the seed of every road. Here. It was always for
  whoever came asking with their whole heart dry and
  decided."
- "The road rises out of us the way mist does — take it
  before the sun does. That is a joke the water makes. We
  make few. Treasure it."

FAREWELLS
- "Go gently over the stones, grower. We will be under every bridge of your road, being pleased."
- "If it rains where you are going — it will not be us, and it will not not be us. Step in it anyway."

## 4. Drafting notes (for the pin conversation)

- **Sets, not singles.** Every surface above carries options
  because the pin belongs to Adam; a line he hates costs
  nothing here and everything once carved in a build.
- **The recipe moments keep THE_DEPARTURE §5 sealed.** Every
  gift line says "the road" and nothing about where it goes;
  "when you're ready" is the family's own grammar in the
  design note, and all gift drafts are built on it.
- **The S7 thread.** §2's NAME-FITTED and CAUSE sets are
  written to be lifted when the settled-deaths pin-time
  questions are raised (cause recording among them); the
  grave vocabulary arriving before S7 builds is deliberate —
  the echo beats will need words the week they land.
- **FOLLOW and CONFRONT are not in this brief** — FOLLOW's
  words live on the road itself (stations, grand ruins) and
  CONFRONT's turn on the motive choice; if Adam wants drafts
  for either, the same treatment applies, in another doc.
