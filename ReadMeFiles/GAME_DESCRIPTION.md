# BioForge — Canonical Game Description

*Provisional canonical — adopted 2026-10-19; later cleanup expected.
This is the single source from which the README, itch.io page,
future store pages, and short public descriptions draw. It replaces
neither the Primer's design pillars nor the longer lore/design
documents; it is the public description of the game as it stands.*

## The one line

**BioForge is a first-person browser game about exploring a living,
seeded world, befriending its creatures, and growing — rather than
building — your tools, gear, and home.**

## Short description

BioForge is a free first-person exploration and crafting game that
plays in your browser. Every world grows from a seed: strange country,
living creatures, ancient ruins, and a sky with its own turning. You
do not manufacture your equipment here. You grow it — tools, weapons,
shields, clothing-like living gear, tunnels, walls, and home itself —
while learning which creatures can be trusted, which plants can be
planted again, and what the vanished Precursors left behind.

Play peacefully, brave a land with teeth, or enter a hostile world
where other explorers are dangerous too. Invite family or friends
into the host's world, and carry your own world between devices with
cloud saves guarded by three words — no account required.

## Standard description

**BioForge** is a first-person game about living in a world that
grows back.

Nothing here is manufactured. There are no factories and no ordinary
workbenches, and even the works of the ancient Precursors are grown
husks rather than machines. Your Bio-Forge cultivates what you need:
harvesting tools, living weapons, shields, structures, storage, and
companions. Your equipment grows with you — a gauntlet for doing, a
helmet for knowing, Quicksteps for going, and a shield for warding.

Each world is generated from a seed, so its country, weather, ruins,
creatures, and dangers belong to that world alone. Cross meadows,
rootwoods, wetlands, ember fields, saltglass flats, storm country,
caverns, and the hybrid lands where two kinds of country meet.
Hundreds of species of plants, fungi, and creatures live by their own
habits. Some can be befriended and brought home; death is permanent
unless a living sample was saved for the Culture Vat, where a creature
can be grown again.

Choose how dangerous the world should be. **Peaceful** keeps death
away. **Normal** gives the wild country teeth outside the safety of
your starting zone. **Hostile** lets explorers harm one another as
well. In co-op, another player can join from their own device and
share the host's world under the host's law.

Above all this turns a strange sky of suns, moons, planets, storms,
and meteor showers. Below and beyond it wait the grown ruins of the
Precursors, their eleven fates, and the long preparation for a
departure that remains a mystery.

BioForge is free, needs no download, and asks for no account. Your
save stays on your device; if you choose, an optional cloud copy can
follow you to another computer or phone behind three secret words
that only you hold.

## Feature lines

- **Grown, not built** — equipment, structures, weapons, and creatures
  come from living cultivation, not factories or machines.
- **A seeded living world** — new country, ruins, weather, and
  creature life generated for every world seed.
- **Creatures with lives** — befriend, follow, protect, hunt, or
  regrow them; individuals matter, and death has weight.
- **Three ways to live** — Peaceful, Normal, and Hostile, with the
  starting zone kept as a refuge in every world.
- **Family co-op** — join the host's world from another device by
  room code or invite; the host's world and difficulty govern all.
- **Plant it back** — species return by seed, spore, cutting, or
  piece according to their own nature; some remain wild alone.
- **Cloud saves without an account** — optional, off until chosen,
  and carried between devices by three words only the player holds.
- **The Precursor mystery** — explore grown ruins, recover what was
  left behind, and prepare for the Departure.

## Accuracy rails

Use these to keep every shortened version honest:

- BioForge is **not** primarily a survival-combat game. Combat exists,
  and the armed modes matter, but cultivation, exploration, and
  creature relations are the heart.
- It is **not** industrial crafting. Nothing important is manufactured;
  the world and its works are grown.
- Co-op is **not** serverless peer-to-peer. Players join a
  host-authoritative world through BioForge's relay.
- Cloud saves are **optional**, local-first, and do not use player
  accounts.
- Do not promise what waits beyond the Departure; the game itself has
  not revealed it.
