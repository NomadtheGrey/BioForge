# BioForge — Player Styles

Seven playstyles the game should reward. No lanes, no tracks, no classes —
one artful listing players can chase whatever calls to them.

## The seven

1. **Green Thumb** — growing, fertilizing, and harvesting. The farming loop:
   planting, compost, fertilizers, mature harvests.
2. **Animal Lovers** — taming, naming, commanding, and keeping companions.
   Soft-puffs, stalkers, and whatever else we add.
3. **Explorers** — seeing the world. Currently thin: the world is 100×100m
   (~30s to cross), so this lane needs a bigger world or hidden points of
   interest before it feels real.
4. **Hunters** — tracking and taking down creatures. *(Unparked
   2026-10-18: hostile fauna and combat exist — Normal arms the
   hunt, Hostile arms explorers against each other. See
   CORE_MECHANICS §4.8.)*
5. **Builders** — raising a base. Structures, floors, mounds, doors,
   layout as self-expression.
6. **Socializers** — playing together. The multiplayer lane: co-op sessions,
   room codes, shared base-building. (The current north star: Adam + daughter.)
7. **Completionists** — filling the codex, discovering every species,
   crafting every recipe, seeing every corner.

## Standing decisions

- Objectives must not pigeon-hole players: **one** special listing
  (page/dialog), not a lane/track system.
- Fix what's in the game first. Objectives stay parked until the game
  deserves them.
- Recipe/economy depth feeds Green Thumbs and Completionists first;
  creature AI feeds Animal Lovers and Hunters; biomes/world-size feed
  Explorers; multiplayer feeds Socializers; build system feeds Builders.

## Recipe hooks per style (as of 2026-10-12)

- Bio-pellets (soft-puff) → growth fertilizer. More pellet recipes later,
  aimed at Explorers and Hunters as new items arrive.

## Candidate additions (brainstorm, 2026-10-13 — NOT approved)

Five styles the original seven may not fully cover. Each is distinct from
an existing style, not a rename of one:

8. **Xenobiologists** — the scientists. Completionists *collect*;
   Xenobiologists want to *understand* — run the growth experiment, observe
   the behavior, decode the world's fate. Fed by the growth/fertilizer
   systems, behavior observation, and the lore layer.
9. **Engineers** — the optimizers. Builders build for self-expression;
   Engineers build for *throughput* — light-grid layouts, collector-pet
   logistics, production chains that hum.
10. **Homesteaders** — the cozy players. Not construction for its own sake:
    making one safe, beautiful *home* in a wild place. Peaceful mode's
    beating heart.
11. **Survivalists** — Hunters want to hunt; Survivalists want to be
    *hunted*. Normal and Hostile modes, world events (the Turning), far
    zones entered deliberately under-geared.
12. **Chroniclers** — the lore-keepers. Explorers go places; Chroniclers
    want places to *mean* something — fates pieced together, ruins read
    like chapters, pets and bases with names and histories.

Deliberately not chased (for now): **Traders** — no player economy exists
to trade in; **Speedrunners** — against this game's whole grain.
